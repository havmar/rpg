# The station's people

- **the Director:** decides who goes out; careful, tired, would rather be wrong in the way the forms allow.
- **the Reader:** sits under the ears and chalks the readings; keeps a notebook she shows nobody.
- **the Clerk:** keeps the board and the forms; knows every seat's day off and is never wrong about it.
- **the Mechanic:** runs the fabricator and the sending room; talks to machines more than to people.
- **the Auditor:** the Board's; counts the readings, the dead and the cost, and writes to Head Office.
- **the Publican:** runs the public house across the yard; hears everything said in the station and repeats none of it.
