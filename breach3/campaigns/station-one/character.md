# The Owl: the pair

Seat: **the Owl**. Rank: Probationer. Level 1, 0 XP (level 2 at 300). Day off: Saturday.
Purse (shared): **36 GP**. Relics: none yet.

## Verity Sallow · Wizard (Evoker) 1 · she/her

Tiefling (Infernal legacy) · Criminal background · Back.
Quirk: refuses to step on a drain cover.

- **HP** 8/8 (d6 per Wizard level; 6 + CON 2). **AC** 13 unarmoured, 16 with Mage Armor.
- **Speed** 9 m. **Darkvision** 18 m. **Initiative** +5 (Alert).
- **Proficiency** +2.
- **Scores:** STR 9 (−1), DEX 16 (+3), CON 15 (+2), INT 16 (+3), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 12 (+1).
  (Criminal background: +1 to DEX, CON and INT.)
- **Saves:** Intelligence +5, Wisdom +4.
- **Skills:** Arcana +5, Investigation +5, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +5, Perception +2
  (passive 12). **Tools:** Thieves' Tools. **Feat:** Alert.
- **Spellcasting:** Intelligence. Spell attack +5, spell save DC 13. Spell slots: 2 of level 1.
  Arcane Recovery once per Long Rest (one level 1 slot back on a Short Rest).
  Ritual Adept: Detect Magic can be cast as a ritual from the book.

**Cantrips (5):** Fire Bolt (Fiendish Legacy: 36 m, 1d10 Fire, +5 to hit), Thaumaturgy
(Otherworldly Presence), Light, Mage Hand, Ray of Frost.

**Prepared (4):** Sleep, Magic Missile, Thunderwave, Mage Armor.

**Spellbook (6 of level 1):** the four above, plus Detect Magic and Feather Fall.

**Fiendish Legacy (Infernal):** Resistance to Fire. Hellish Rebuke at level 3, Darkness at
level 5, each castable once free per Long Rest or with a slot.

**Evoker:** Evocation Savant and Potent Cantrip at level 3 (she chooses the two Evocation spells
at level 3). Sculpt Spells at 6, Empowered Evocation at 10,
Overchannel at 14.

**Weapons:** 4 Daggers (1d4 Piercing, Finesse, Light, Thrown 6/18 m, Nick). +5 to hit,
1d4+3 Piercing. Simple weapons only.

**Carries:** spellbook (3 lb), quarterstaff (Arcane Focus), robe, 4 daggers, Thieves' Tools,
crowbar, 2 pouches, traveler's clothes, Scholar's Pack (backpack, book, ink, ink pen, lamp,
10 flasks of oil, 10 sheets of parchment, tinderbox). **Gold:** 21 GP (in the purse).

## Abbott · Barbarian (Berserker) 1 · he/him

Automaton, Medium, Front. Bound to Verity: level 1, gains no XP. Protocol: History and
Tinker's Tools.
Quirk: polishes the needle's glass while he waits.

- **HP** 14/14 (d12 per Barbarian level; 12 + CON 2). **AC** 14: 13 unarmoured
  (Unarmored Defense, 10 + DEX 1 + CON 2), +1 Integrated Protection.
- **Speed** 9 m. **Initiative** +1. **Proficiency** +2.
- **Scores:** STR 15 (+2), DEX 13 (+1), CON 14 (+2), WIS 10 (+0), CHA 10 (+0), INT 8 (−1).
- **Saves:** Strength +4, Constitution +4.
- **Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +2, History +1 (protocol). **Tools:** Tinker's Tools.

**Rage:** 2 uses per Long Rest (1 back on a Short Rest), +2 damage with Strength attacks.
Bonus Action to enter. Lasts to the end of his next turn; extend with a Bonus Action, up to
10 minutes. No Heavy armour.

**Weapon Mastery:** Cleave (Greataxe), Vex (Handaxe).

**Weapons:**
- Greataxe (Heavy, Two-Handed): +4 to hit, 1d12+2 Slashing (+2 while raging).
- 4 Handaxes (Light, Thrown 6/18 m): +4 to hit, 1d6+2 Slashing.

**Automaton:** Constructed Resilience (advantage against Poisoned, Resistance to Poison,
immune to disease, needs no food, drink or air, doesn't sleep). Sentry's Rest (a Long Rest
is 6 hours, motionless and aware). Needle: a brass compass the size of a saucer, coupled to
his core, set at the station to the reading he is sent to. His **core** (1 kg) holds his
memory while Verity lives.

**Carries:** greataxe, 4 handaxes, Explorer's Pack, Tinker's Tools, the needle. No armour
(Integrated Protection is part of the body).
