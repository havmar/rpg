# Chronicle · Saltmarket Precinct

The station's record, in the GM's voice. Each call in a line or two; each death, each trouble.

## Day 1, Monday

The Owl's first week. A Probationer, Verity Sallow, and an automaton, Abbott, take the seat
the Board has held for them since Sunday.

**6 am, the board.** The Reader chalks a reading of under one for Gasworks. The Director sends
the Owl, the lowest seat free to take it. The needle points north-east, past the gasholder.

**The platform.** The Owl walks out through the workhouse queue and past the gasholder to a
goods platform behind the siding. Two Gray Oozes (CR 1/2 each) are on the boards. They have
seen the pair and have not attacked. Abbott proposes to knock them out and pen them.

**The fight, and the kill.** The human chooses to kill them for the XP, not bring them in.
Verity's two Magic Missiles and Abbott's raging handaxes wear them down; a dagger finishes
the first, and a second dagger the last, on the fourth round. Nobody is hurt. XP 200, 20 GP,
nothing left behind. The Owl is still a Probationer, and 100 XP short of level 2.

**Lunch and the evening drink.** The other seats had no calls. The canteen talks the morning
over in a line or two. At the public house the Clerk reads the board: two 8s in Gasworks
and a 2 in Brick Fields, waiting for the morning. The Wren is the only seat that can take
the eights. The Owl sleeps through the night on a Long Rest.

## Day 2, Tuesday

The Owl is not called: the board has nothing at its level. The other seats work the day.
The Wren sends an Assassin home (read 8), then kills a Frost Giant (8), a Stone Golem (10) and a
Fire Giant (9), and comes in hurt from the last. The Fox brings in an Ogre (2) and it is sent
home. The Lion kills a Chimera (6), then comes back from Printers' Row without the Giant
Crocodile (5), which is still out there. At the evening drink two more readings are chalked
in Soap Lane: a 7 and a 4.

## Day 3, Wednesday

**6 am.** Readings of 3 (Brick Fields) and 9 (Vinegar Yard) are chalked. The Lion goes back
out for the Giant Crocodile, the Fox for the 4 in Soap Lane. The Wren stays in, hurt. The 7, the
3 and the 9 wait. The Owl is not called.
