# GM secrets · Saltmarket Precinct

The GM's. The human doesn't read this file.

## Why the breaches happen

The mast stands on the old crossing where the Saltmarket's closed railway tunnels run into the
Under. Beneath its footings is a seam, a place where another world's ground has always pressed
close to this one. The ears were built to hear the seam, and by listening they hold it at a
pitch. The breaches come at the rate the Board can carry: four pairs, four dice a dawn and
four a dusk. Nobody at the station has noticed that the readings track the Board's budget
and not the weather.

## What the sending room does

It sends a thing home through the seam, and it works. But each sending leaves the seam a
hair wider, and the fabricator ticks faster each year. Trouble is a thing failing to find
the seam on its own: it is too narrow for what it carries, so the thing brings its own world
with it into the street.

## The veterans at Head Office

A holder called to Head Office at level 12 is not retired. Their automaton's core is set
into the seam as an anchor, and the holder goes down with it, in the closed van, and the
seat is refilled by a Probationer on the next morning. The core is the thing that holds the
seam at pitch; the bond the Board calls "continuity of equipment" is the same bond, kept in a
wall. The wall lists only the dead. A called veteran has no line on it, which the Clerk
notices first.

## Why the Owl

The Board's admission test measured how much a body holds another world's pull without
leaking it. Verity's Infernal result is what got her onto the list. Abbott's core was chosen
for the same reason. The Board does not want the Owl to fail; it wants the Owl to grow.

## Clues, in roughly the order they should turn up

1. The Reader's notebook: her readings follow the day's dice, not the sky.
2. The Auditor's ledger: "called to Head Office" is a longer column than "retired".
3. The fabricator ticks on days with no sendings.
4. A stranded thing in Vinegar Yard that knows the way to the seam by smell and cannot say why.
5. The wall: a seat's name with no day beside it.

## Ways out, for the players to find

- Stop the ears: Head Office won't allow it, and the Board's people can't tell it's needed.
- Send things home through the seam: it works, and it widens the seam.
- Walk into the seam: someone must become the anchor, and the Board has a list of who.
- Keep the station running until the Board needs a different answer: the station's own
  method, the pairs' habit of bringing things in instead of killing them.
