## Who she is

Lean, weathered brown skin, copper hair cut short with a knife, a nicked left eyebrow.
Grey leathers with too many pockets; always eating something out of one of them. Quiet,
polite, counts things, answers a question with a question, keeps her word because a
reputation is a tool. Wants a door that's hers, owing nobody rent, tithe or protection.
Came upriver four months ago after a warehouse job went wrong and her fence talked.
"Who has the key? No, I'm only asking."
