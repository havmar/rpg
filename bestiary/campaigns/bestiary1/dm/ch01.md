# Chapter 1 prep · Day 1 in Lessing

## The omen: Water Elemental (CR 5) as an NPC

Reading chosen: **the river is a tenant too, and it's on strike.** Old Tamber, the
river, keeps the ford shallow; the ford is why Lessing is here and the toll (900 GP a
year) is a quarter of the rent. Since the first captains camped here, the town has poured
a cask of southern red into the river at the Feast and said thank you. Two years ago,
saving money, the council (Ostry's motion) cut the cask. Nobody told the river. At dawn
on Day 1 the ford runs deep for the first time in 80 years. A wool caravan (Ostry's) is
stuck on the south bank.

Second reading considered: the elemental is what the miners broke into in the Old Tally.
Kept as a later possibility (the river's "cellar"); don't spend it now.

- **Old Tamber**: rises at the ferry landing as a tall shape of brown water when spoken
  to in Primordial, or by the Dunmores, who keep the old words. Speaks Aquan only. Slow,
  literal, not angry: offended. Int 5: it doesn't understand money, it understands
  promises. **Want:** its cask, and to be asked properly, by someone who means it.
  Two years owed. **Won't:** let the ford down for a sharp tongue or a threat.
- Attitude: **Indifferent** to strangers, **Hostile** to Ostrys. Influence DC 15. Wine
  in hand: advantage. An Ostry present: disadvantage.
- Talk needs Aquan: Clemency Dunmore knows the old words (a few phrases); Maud has
  *Comprehend Languages* (understand only, ritual 11 min). Tick has nothing.
- A cask of southern red: 10 GP (DM's price; the SRD has none). The Lantern has one. Gil
  will sell it for 12.
- If it's fixed: Clemency Dunmore owes the party, and the ford toll is saved (900 GP a
  year). Ostry is embarrassed. 150 XP for the deed (a deal that won what a Low fight would).
- If not: the toll is lost until someone fixes it; Ostry blames the commons' strike in
  public, Garrow blames Ostry.

## The Old Tally · omen: Wyvern (CR 6) as a place

Reading: **the Old Tally is now a wyvern's lair, a squatter in the founders' property.**
- Two years ago the deep level fell into a natural cavern below (bad air, collapse):
  eleven dead. No monster; a mine accident, and the founders saved on props (Wenna
  knows the props were cut too).
- The collapse ran up a fault to the hilltop, and last winter the hilltop fell in: a
  sinkhole 20 m across, a deep sheltered warm pit open to the sky. A wyvern pushed south
  by the Landlady's range took it (a lesser dragon is "not worse than herself"; the
  charter doesn't keep it out). The pit floor IS the mine's main hall, under the old
  shaft. The strongroom opens off the main hall.
- Last spring's two crews went in by the adit and met it in the hall. One crew of four
  from downriver; one local crew of three including **Daff Pike, Garrow's nephew**,
  hired by Ostry at 10 GP a head. Their gear is in the hall among bones.
- **The wyvern's day:** hunts at first light and at dusk (sheep, deer; shepherds east of
  town have lost 30 sheep since winter), sleeps in the pit from late morning to dusk,
  and at night. Passive Perception 14, darkvision 36 m. Heist window: dusk (about 19:00)
  for about 1 to 2 hours while it hunts. Or a stealth job while it sleeps (Stealth vs
  14).
- **The strongroom:** iron door, good lock (Thieves' Tools DC 15), keys long lost with
  the foreman. Inside: **800 GP of silver ingots = 72 kg** (16 ingots of 4.5 kg, 50 GP
  each). The party can't carry it all (Tick has ~34 kg spare, Maud ~30): a mine cart on
  the rails to the adit (rusted; Athletics DC 12 to get it moving, loud).
- **In the nest:** one wyvern **egg** (warm, 6 kg, the size of a barrel-head). Rent in
  kind? Ibbet would take it: the Landlady likes curiosities, and a wyvern egg brought to
  her is a joke she'd enjoy (worth 400 GP to Ibbet). Taking it brings the wyvern hunting
  to Lessing, in rage, which the charter doesn't stop. Real choice.
- Crews' gear: 4 sets of remains: a longsword, a chain shirt (bitten), 31 GP and 40 SP
  scattered, Daff's lucky tin whistle with his name scratched on it (for Garrow), and a
  Potion of Climbing (Common).
- Fight if it happens: Wyvern 2,300 XP vs a 800 High budget: Deadly. Run, hide, the
  adit (it's Large; it can enter the 2-square adit but can't fly in it).
