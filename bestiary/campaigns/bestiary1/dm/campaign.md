# Campaign prep · bestiary1

## The engine in one paragraph

A town that rents its valley from a dragon is 1,550 GP short with 29 days to go. If the
rent fails, a devil takes about twenty people of three founding families, and the dragon
takes everything inside the walls. The people who aren't on the charter have paid the
head-tax for 80 years to keep the founders' necks safe, and this year they won't. A
devil's notary, quite open about what he is, is offering loans. The party: a thief who
left home to stop paying rent, and a wizard whose name is on the charter.

The question the campaign asks: **who pays, and with what?** Gold from the marches, the
commons' tax, souls through a loan, the founders' blood, or something nobody has thought
of yet (the charter rewritten, the dragon persuaded, the House outplayed). Nobody here is
a villain except, professionally, Sallow, and even he has a reason.

## The factions

### The Rent Office (the Landlady) · seed omen: Adult Red Dragon, faction

- **People:** the Landlady (never seen in town); **Ibbet Crane**, steward; kobolds at the
  Burnt Road toll-house.
- **Want:** the rent, on time. The Landlady likes the arrangement: 80 years of gold and
  no wars. But she'd lose nothing much by a default either: she gets the town's goods and
  is free of the charter. She is **indifferent to which way it goes, and curious**.
- **Ibbet** is not indifferent. She came to Lessing at 20 and has buried friends here.
  She can't extend the deadline, can't lend, can't lie on the books. She **can appraise
  rent in kind**, and the Landlady loves curiosities. A magic item, a work of art, a
  thing with a story is worth more to her than its price (up to double the SRD price,
  Ibbet's call, and more for something the Landlady particularly wants).
- **What the Landlady particularly wants (secret, for later):** the Margrave's banner,
  which went into the Glass Fields with his army and wasn't in the ashes. She has wanted
  it for 80 years, as a trophy. The charter bars her from going south to look for anyone
  who might have taken it. (Ibbet knows; she'll say so only to someone she trusts, or
  someone who asks the right question about rent in kind.)
- **Means:** absolute. Not used unless the charter breaks.
- **Plan if nobody stops anything:** Day 30 at sundown, Ibbet counts the box. If it's
  short she writes DEFAULT in the ledger. At midnight the Erinyes arrives. At dawn the
  Landlady comes south.
- **Opinion of the party:** none yet. Ibbet likes people who pay on time and don't
  haggle.

### The Charterholders · the council

- **Aldous Ferrant**, mayor, 71: courteous, tired, ashamed. He has five great-
  grandchildren. He won't take the loan on others' souls, but he is wavering, and he
  hides that from Maud. Voice: long sentences that end in apologies.
- **Hesketh Ostry**, 50s, wool: practical to the point of cruelty, and honest about it.
  "The commons will lose one soul in ten households over thirty years. We lose all of
  our children in one night. Do the sums." He'll push the loan. Won't: lie about what it
  costs. Voice: numbers, shortness.
- **Clemency Dunmore**, 40s, the ferry: the swing vote. Plain, shrewd, distrusts Ostry,
  likes Wenna Tasker more than she'll admit. She'd vote against the loan if anyone showed
  her another way to 1,550 GP.
- **Want:** the rent paid; their blood safe.
- **Means:** the council (law, the watch of six Guards, the Tally House), money (between
  them ~300 GP they can still raise), bounties.
- **Bounty posted on Day 1:** the Old Tally strongroom's ingots (800 GP of silver),
  "recovered to the Moot Hall: 100 GP and one in ten of the recovered" (so 180 GP).
- **Plan:** Day 18, council vote on Sallow's loan; passes 2-1 (Ostry, Ferrant) unless
  the box is clearly heading for 4,000 or Ferrant is given a reason. Day 20, the head-tax:
  if the strike holds they send the watch to collect, which turns ugly.

### The Commons · seed omen: Commoner, enemy

- **Wenna Tasker**, 30, tanner, stained to the elbow, funny, angry. Her brother Pell died
  in the Old Tally, which the Ferrants, Ostrys and Dunmores owned. She and Maud were
  friends as girls. **Want:** a seat at the charter. If the commons pay, the commons sign
  and get a say. **Won't:** let children be hurt, of any family. That is where her
  movement will split from her.
- **Garrow Pike**, 50s, a carter, the hard edge of the Commons: let the founders burn,
  and if the watch comes for the tax, the watch can burn too.
- **The Commons as an enemy:** not monsters, a crowd. Commoners (CR 0, club +2) and a few
  Toughs and Bandits among the carters. In a fight they are a mob, and they are people:
  every one hurt has a name the town knows.
- **Means:** numbers (most of the town), the strike (about 500 GP withheld), Sallow's
  free legal advice (which is true: the commons aren't liable under the charter).
- **Plan:** the strike holds to Day 20. If the loan passes on Day 18 and is secured on
  "the people of Lessing", Garrow's people storm the Moot Hall on Day 19 to burn the
  loan papers. Wenna tries to stop the storming and fails, unless helped.
- **What would turn them:** a written promise of a seat at the charter's renewal (the
  charter can be amended only with the Landlady's and the House's consent, at a payment:
  Ibbet knows this), or the founders putting their own money in first.

### The House of Accounts · seed omen: Erinyes, enemy

- **Corvin Sallow**, 40s, Witness and notary, human. Grey coat, ink on the right
  forefinger, very good manners. Open about what he is; the town uses him for wills
  because he never cheats. **He cannot lie in writing.** He can lie aloud and mostly
  doesn't bother. His own soul belongs to the House (a debt of his father's, inherited);
  he is promised it back after one great acquisition. A default (20 souls of founders'
  blood) would do it; a loan on the town would do it twice over.
- **Nib**, his imp clerk (Imp, CR 1), usually a raven on the sign. Spiteful, gossips,
  will sell small secrets for sugar and for insults to Sallow.
- **Want:** a default or, better, a loan.
- **Won't:** hinder the payment (the charter would give him to the Erinyes himself). He
  can make offers, give true advice, lend, and buy.
- **The loan:** 1,600 GP at once, to the council, "secured upon the people of Lessing":
  on any year's failure to repay 200 GP, one soul per ten households, chosen by lot, for
  thirty years. He'll also lend to individuals, small, on their own souls, with good
  terms written plainly.
- **Plan:** Day 3 he puts the loan to the council in writing. He lends Wenna's people
  whatever they need to hold the strike (food money, interest-free, secured on nothing:
  a gift, because the strike serves him). He's at the payment on Day 30 either way.
- **The Erinyes:** arrives only at breach, through the Witness's door, at midnight.
  Not a fight. Through omens she may be felt earlier (dreams of the rope, a burn on the
  charter's counterpart).

## The party

### Maud Ferrant (the companion)

- Aldous's granddaughter, 24. Back two months from the college in the south, where she
  studied evocation; came home because of the rent. Her name is on the charter by
  blood.
- **Want:** the rent paid without the loan. Deeper: her life not owed to anyone on a
  signature she never made. She has a copy of the charter and reads it at night looking
  for a door.
- **Won't:** take the House's money, even to save herself. Strike at a commoner who
  hasn't struck first.
- **Manner:** counts everything (squares, slots, coins), short sentences, dry. Some
  college words she's embarrassed by. An owl familiar, **Clerk**. Fond of her
  grandfather, furious with him for being afraid.
- **Wenna** was her best friend at twelve. They haven't spoken since Pell died.
- **On Tick:** Maud hired her. She needs someone who can open the Old Tally strongroom,
  and she doesn't trust the council's hired swords. She doesn't yet know how Tick feels
  about rent.

### Tick Marrow (the PC, the player's)

- A thief who left home to stop paying rent, in a town that exists by paying rent. Her
  want, a door that's hers, owing nobody, is exactly what the commons want and what the
  charter forbids. She'll understand Wenna at once; she's travelling with Maud.
- Hooks: she wants to know how much is in the box and who counts it (Ibbet; Sallow
  witnesses); she can read Draconic (the charter's dragon-side text is in Draconic, and
  the copy in the Rent Office has a clause the Common copy doesn't: amendment "by
  payment of a thing the Lessor desires"). Her fence downriver talked, so someone from
  downriver may come looking for her: a good use for a future NPC omen.
- Don't steer her. Let the world make offers: Sallow will offer her a loan for a house.
  A house with her name on the deed, Lessing land, which means a lease from the
  Landlady. The question of whether you can ever really own a door here is the whole
  campaign in small.

## Chapter 1 opening (Day 1)

- Morning, the Lantern. Maud has hired Tick (agreed fee: 20 GP and a share of the
  bounty). The bounty notice on the board. The party is rested.
- What's happening: Wenna stands on a table in the common room at noon to announce the
  strike. Garrow is loud behind her. A Ferrant at the table makes it personal; Wenna and
  Maud see each other.
- Sallow is in the corner having breakfast, polite to everyone; Nib on the rafters.
- Ibbet Crane at the Rent Office counts the box every morning at the window, openly.
  Anyone can watch.
- The omen at chapter start sets the scene's extra colour; then a second omen when the
  party sets out for the mine (or wherever they go).
