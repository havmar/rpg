## Who she is

24, the mayor's granddaughter, back two months from the college in the south. Ink on her
cuffs, hair pinned with a pencil. Counts everything. Short sentences, dry, now and then a
college word she's embarrassed by. Her name is on the charter by blood. She won't take
the devil's money, even to save herself.
