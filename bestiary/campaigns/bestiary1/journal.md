# Tick Marrow · journal

## Day 1 · 1st of Reaping · rent day is the Feast, first frost (about Day 30)

**What happened.** Maud Ferrant hired me three days ago: 20 GP when the job's done, and a
share of the Old Tally bounty. This morning the ford had gone deep and still without any
rain. A strike notice was nailed to the Rent Office door. We stopped the river, got the
silver and came home at 22:45 with all sixteen bars. The bars are in Maud's room at the
Ferrant house (green door, on the square). We counted them aloud, and the count is on a
scrap signed by both of us. They go to the Moot Hall when it opens: 100 GP + a tenth
(80) = 180 GP.

**The rent box.** I watched the steward count it through the window: 17 stacks of 100,
so **1,700 GP**. Everyone says the rent is 4,000. A woman in spectacles counts it every
morning in plain view.

**The strike.** Notice to the Houses of Ferrant, Ostry and Dunmore from 97 households:
no ground-rent from the **20th of Reaping** until the commons get a seat and a signature
at the Charter. Signed W. Tasker. Founders own the ground and the commons pay them rent,
and the founders pay the dragon. So the box gets shorter still.

**My debt, on my own word.** The river (a water elemental, CR 5, lives in the ford) gave
the ford for a cup of southern red and a thank-you every Feast. The council cut the cup
two years ago to save 10 GP; it was Hesketh Ostry's motion. It asked for two cups. One
was poured today (Maud paid 12 GP). **I swore the second comes on the wine barge, about
Day 10, and that the cup is poured every Feast from now on, whatever the council says.**
The river said: "The small one's word I keep." Gil at the Lantern holds the first cask
off the barge for me: 2 GP down, slate "MARROW · river" on the cellar door. I still owe
him the other 10 or so. DON'T MISS THE BARGE.

**People**
- **Maud Ferrant**: my employer, the mayor's granddaughter. Counts things, keeps her
  word, paid for the cask without a speech, carried 45 kg uphill without a complaint.
  Her grandfather signed the order that killed the miners. She read it to the end anyway.
  I like her. Owl: Clerk.
- **A. Ferrant**: the mayor, her grandfather. Not met yet.
- **Corvin Sallow**, "the Witness": grey coat, ink on his right finger. Keeps the
  charter for the House of Accounts in **Dis** (devils). Does wills; did the grandfather's.
  **He wrote the strike notice for free.** Why would the devils' clerk help short the box?
- **Wenna Tasker**: tanner, about 30, leads the strike. Maud's childhood friend. Her
  brother **Pell** died in the Old Tally. Thinks I'm on the Ferrants' rent. Fair.
- **Clemency Dunmore**: owns the ferry, kept the river's words. Voted against cutting
  the cup. **Says she owes me and doesn't forget debts.** Good friend to have.
- **Hesketh Ostry**: founder, wool caravans (11 wagons, 40 GP toll). He cut the cup and
  hired the dead local lads at 10 GP a head. Will claim he fixed the ford. Let him.
- **Garrow Pike**: big loud carter. His nephew Daff died in the mine.
- **Gil**: keeps the Lantern.

**The Old Tally (8 km east, 2 hours).** It was no monster two years ago. The council
ordered props at four yards, not two, to save money. There was warm water and bad air,
Ostry said "keep on", and the floor fell into a hole that was already there. Eleven
died. Now a **WYVERN** (CR 6, far too much for us) lives in the fallen hall with **one
egg**. It hunts at dawn and dusk, takes the eastern shepherds' sheep (30 since winter)
and screeched when it came home. We left the egg alone. The strongroom is empty now.

**The daybook (foreman Hobb Garside).** I have it.
- 4th of Sowing: council letter under seal, props at four yards, signed
  **H. Ostry, C. Dunmore, A. Ferrant, mayor**. Garside protested and was overruled.
- 9th: warm water and bad air; told Ostry; "keep on".
- 11th: sixteen bars to the strongroom. "Pay Friday." Then nothing.
- This could break all three founding houses, and the strike would love it. Careful who
  sees it. Clemency signed too.

**To return to the families (one pocket):** Daff Pike's tin whistle (to Garrow), the tag
**R. VOSS · TAMBERLUND LEVY**, and **Ansel's letter "Dear Mam"** to his mother in
Tamberlund.

**What I carry.** 73 GP, a Potion of Climbing, the Bead of Nourishment, 6 flasks of oil
(used one on the cart), rations, everything else on my sheet. Longstrider unused.

**Next.**
1. Ingots to the Moot Hall, with witnesses. Get the 180 and my 20 GP.
2. Return the whistle, tag and letter.
3. Decide what to do with the daybook. Ask Maud first: her family's name is on it.
4. Find out why Sallow helps the strike, and what happens if the box is short at the
   Feast.
5. The barge, about Day 10. The cask. My word.

**Worries.** A short box and a dragon landlady. A devil's clerk who's being generous. A
wyvern 8 km away that's just found its silver gone. And I gave my word to a river.
