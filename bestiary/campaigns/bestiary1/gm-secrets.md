# bestiary1 · the world as made

## The history

- 80 years ago the Margrave of Tamberlund marched north to kill the red dragon of the
  Chimney. She burned his army on the plain now called **the Glass Fields** (25 km N).
- The survivors' camp at the Tamber ford was led by three captains: **Ferrant, Ostry and
  Dunmore**. They made terms with her: rent for the valley. Neither side trusted the
  other, so the charter was notarised in **Dis**, by the House of Accounts, the one notary
  both would accept. The camp became **Lessing**.
- **The Charter of Lessing**, in short:
  - The Lessees are "the houses of Ferrant, Ostry and Dunmore, and their blood after
    them", who answer for the rent with their persons.
  - Rent: 4,000 GP a year, in coin or in kind as the Lessor's steward appraises it,
    delivered into the steward's hands at the Rent Office by sundown on the Feast of
    Embers (first frost).
  - The Lessor (the dragon) does not harm the town or its people, and lets nothing worse
    than herself come south of the Burnt Road toll-house. She doesn't come south of it
    herself except on default.
  - On default: the Lessees' blood are forfeit to the House of Accounts, collected by its
    enforcer, and the Lessor takes "all goods within the walls".
  - A Witness of the House of Accounts sits in Lessing and must be present at payment.
    He may not hinder it.
  - Either party breaking the terms is forfeit to the House's enforcer. (The dragon is
    bound too, which is why she has been a good landlady for 80 years.)

## Lessing

- About 600 people, on the ford of the river **Tamber**. A palisade, a stone gatehouse,
  grey-stone houses with slate roofs. No thatch allowed, by town law since the dragon's
  one warning fire in year 12.
- **The Square:** the Rent Office (squat stone, iron door, a brass claw plaque), the Moot
  Hall, and across from the Rent Office the Witness's office: a neat painted sign,
  "Sallow, Notary. Wills, Deeds, Loans Arranged".
- **The Tally House** by the gate: where expeditions sell their finds. Assessor **Dobbin
  Reede**, a fat honest clerk, takes the Fifth for the rent box and buys at the SRD's
  sell price (half).
- **The Lantern** (inn, by the ford), run by **Gil Harrowby**: rooms 5 SP a night,
  adventurers' notice board.
- **Tasker's tannery** downriver, stinking, where the Commons meet.

## The rent box (as of Day 1)

- In it: **2,450 GP**. Needed: **4,000 GP**. Short: **1,550 GP**.
- Why: the **Old Tally silver mine** (8 km E), which paid ~1,500 GP a year in silver
  tax, closed two years ago after the deep level broke into something. Eleven miners died,
  among them Pell Tasker.
- To come: the head-tax (1 GP a household, roughly 500 GP), due on Day 20; the Fifth on
  whatever adventurers sell.
- **The Old Tally strongroom** still holds the last two months' smelted ingots, worth
  **800 GP**. Nobody has gone back for them. What else is down there is set by the omen
  when the party goes.

## The calendar

- Day 1 is the 1st of Reaping. The Feast of Embers, rent day, is **Day 30**.

## The marches (distance from Lessing; the farther, the worse)

- **Near (under 10 km):** the Old Tally mine (8 km E). The Tamber mills. Gallows Hill.
- **Middle (10 to 20 km):** the Fenwick barrows (12 km SW). The Burnt Road toll-house
  (15 km N), kept for the Landlady by kobolds. Ashby, a drowned village (18 km
  downriver).
- **Far (20 km and more):** the Glass Fields (25 km N), the Margrave's burned army and,
  by legend, his pay chest. The Chimney (30 km N).

## People and factions

See `dm/campaign.md` for who wants what and the plans. The facts:

- **The Landlady** (Adult Red Dragon, CR 17). In the Chimney. Old, proud, a collector of
  curiosities. Has kept the charter for 80 years.
- **Ibbet Crane**, her steward, 60s, human, at the Rent Office for 40 years.
- **The Charterholders:** **Aldous Ferrant** (mayor, 71; Maud's grandfather), **Hesketh
  Ostry** (wool merchant, 50s), **Clemency Dunmore** (widow, runs the ferry, 40s). Their
  blood, about 20 people, Maud among them, is what the charter takes.
- **The Commons:** **Wenna Tasker**, tanner, 30, leads the tax strike. Her brother Pell
  died in the Old Tally.
- **Corvin Sallow**, Witness of the House of Accounts, human, 40s, in Lessing six years.
  His clerk is an imp, **Nib**, usually a raven.
- **The Erinyes** (CR 12), the House's enforcer, comes only at a breach.
