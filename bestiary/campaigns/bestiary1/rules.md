# bestiary1 · rules for this campaign

## Rolled at the new game (Day 0)

- **PC class:** d12 = 11 → Back list 5: **Rogue (Thief)**.
- **Companion class:** d12 = 7 → Back list 1: **Wizard (Evoker)**.
- **Scores:** rolled, 4d6 keep the highest 3, six times each.
  - PC, first set: 11, 7, 12, 11, 10, 7 (modifiers total −3). **Rerolled** by the DM's
    ruling below: 12, 9, 12, 15, 13, 11.
  - Companion: 15, 13, 16, 9, 14, 11.

## Rulings

- **Weak set reroll:** a set of rolled scores whose modifiers total less than +1 is
  rerolled whole. (The SRD is silent; a common table rule. Applies to any future rolled
  character.)
- **Duplicate tool proficiency** from a background: take another tool of the same kind
  instead (Tick: Poisoner's Kit).
- **Starting at level 3:** each character gets 1 Common magic item (SRD, Starting at
  Higher Levels), picked with `loot.py --rarity Common`: Tick, Bead of Nourishment;
  Maud, Potion of Healing.
- **Units:** metres and kilograms throughout (SRD feet ÷ 5 × 1.5 for squares; lb × 0.45).
