## Chapter 1 · Day 1 · The river and the silver

- Omens: Water Elemental (NPC), Wyvern (Place).
- The commons' strike notice on the Rent Office door, in Sallow's hand. Tick counted the
  box: 1,700. Maud and Wenna Tasker spoke for the first time in two years.
- The ford stood still: Old Tamber, offended at the two Feast casks the council cut.
  Clemency poured one cask; Tick promised the second on the barge (~Day 10) and the river
  "keeps the small one's word". Ford open. 150 XP.
- The Old Tally: the hilltop has fallen in and a wyvern nests in the mine hall, with an
  egg. Scouted while it slept; at dusk while it hunted, Tick opened the strongroom: all 16
  ingots (800 GP) carried back. Egg left alone. Took the dead crews' things for their
  families. 450 XP.
- The foreman's daybook: the council (Ostry, Dunmore, Ferrant) halved the props two
  years ago, and Ostry ordered work to go on in bad air. Maud read her grandfather's name.
- Open: deliver the silver (Moot Hall?); the daybook; Garrow's nephew's whistle; the
  cask by Day 10; Sallow's loan to the council on Day 3.
