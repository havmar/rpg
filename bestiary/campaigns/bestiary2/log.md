# bestiary2 · log

## Chapter 1 · Day 1 · The white wolf of Frost Hollow

- Omen: Winter Wolf → place, its den in Frost Hollow, 15 km north.
- Hilde met Oda at the Ford Inn; Bel the shepherd told of the tracks; Gerd the trapper
  offered 150 GP for an unburnt pelt; Joss the trapper was missing.
- Hilde bought a ewe for bait, summoned an owl, scouted the den: a grey wolf awake, the
  winter wolf asleep.
- Lured and killed the grey wolf. The winter wolf woke and talked; Hilde lied her way into
  baiting it with the ewe. Web, cold breath, Sleep, Oda's crit and Action Surge; it fled
  and died to Magic Missile at the cave mouth. 750 XP. No one dropped.
- Oda tore the pelt skinning it. Hanna the reeve paid 100 GP, Gerd 120 GP; split evenly.
- Joss's bones and coin returned to Gerd; he gave Hilde the unknown bronze ring. Lodge
  Friendly.
- Open: the ring (Ring of Jumping, unidentified); Hanna has more jobs; Stonefield
  shrine, Copper Hill mine.
