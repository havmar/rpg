# Oda Fenn · the companion (played by the DM)

Human (Medium), Fighter 3 (Champion), Soldier background. She/her, about 30.
XP 900. Proficiency +2.

**Who she is:** ten years a pikeman in a river lord's company, paid off when the war
ended. Few words, blunt, dry. Loyal once she's decided you're worth it.
**Wants:** 300 GP to buy a river boat of her own and never answer to anyone again.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **HP** 31 (10+3, then 6+3, 6+3). Hit Dice 3d10.
- **AC** 16 (Chain Mail).
- **Speed** 9 m (6 squares).
- **Initiative** +3 with Advantage (Alert, Remarkable Athlete; may swap initiative with
  a willing ally).
- **Saves:** STR +6, CON +5 (proficient); DEX +1, INT −1, WIS −1, CHA −2.
- **Skills:** Athletics +6 (Advantage), Intimidation +0, Perception +1, Survival +1,
  Insight +1. Passive Perception 11.
- **Tools:** Dice set. **Languages:** Common, Halfling.

## Attacks

- **Greatsword** +6, 2d6+4 Slashing (Great Weapon Fighting: 1s and 2s count as 3).
  Mastery Graze: on a miss, 4 Slashing damage.
- **Javelin** ×8, +6, 1d6+4 Piercing, thrown 9/36 m. Mastery Slow: a hit cuts the
  target's speed by 3 m until her next turn.
- **Shortbow** +3, 1d6+1 Piercing, 24/96 m. Mastery Vex: a hit gives her Advantage on
  her next attack against that target.
- Spear and Flail carried, not mastered.

## Features

- **Fighting Style:** Great Weapon Fighting. **Weapon Mastery:** Greatsword, Javelin,
  Shortbow.
- **Second Wind** 2/Long Rest (1 back on a Short Rest): Bonus Action, regain 1d10+3 HP.
- **Tactical Mind:** spend a Second Wind on a failed ability check, +1d10.
- **Action Surge** 1/Short Rest.
- **Champion:** Improved Critical (crit on 19–20); Remarkable Athlete (Advantage on
  Initiative and Athletics; after a crit, move half speed without provoking).
- **Human:** Resourceful (Heroic Inspiration on a Long Rest), Skillful (Perception),
  Versatile (Alert). **Soldier:** Savage Attacker (once a turn, roll weapon damage dice
  twice, take either).
