# Oda Fenn · the companion (played by the DM)

Human (Medium), Monk 3 (Warrior of the Open Hand), Criminal background. She/her, about 30.
XP 900. Proficiency +2.

**Who she is:** a former river smuggler who did two years in a cell, then learned the open
hand at a mountain abbey. Few words, blunt, dry. Loyal once she's decided you're worth it.
**Wants:** 300 GP to buy a boat of her own and never answer to anyone again.

## Scores (rolled 16, 6, 15, 13, 8, 8; Criminal +2 DEX, +1 CON)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **HP** 24 (8+2, then 7, 7). Hit Dice 3d8.
- **AC** 16 (Unarmored Defense 10 + DEX + WIS).
- **Speed** 12 m (8 squares; Unarmored Movement +3 m).
- **Initiative** +6 (Alert: + proficiency; may swap initiative with a willing ally).
- **Saves:** STR +1, DEX +6 (proficient); CON +2, INT −1, WIS +2, CHA −2.
- **Skills:** Acrobatics +6, Stealth +6, Sleight of Hand +6, Insight +4, Perception +4,
  Survival +4, Medicine +4, Athletics +1. Passive Perception 14.
- **Tools:** Thieves' Tools +6, Flute.
- **Languages:** Common, Halfling (from her river years).

## Attacks

- **Unarmed Strike** +6, 1d6+4 Bludgeoning (or Grapple/Shove, DC 14 by DEX).
- **Spear** +6, 1d6+4 Piercing (Martial Arts die); thrown 6/18 m.
- **Dagger** +6, 1d6+4 Piercing (Martial Arts die); thrown 6/18 m.

## Features

- **Martial Arts:** d6; Bonus Action Unarmed Strike; DEX for attacks.
- **Focus Points 3** (Short Rest). Save DC 12.
  - Flurry of Blows (1 FP): two Unarmed Strikes as a Bonus Action.
  - Patient Defense: Disengage as BA; 1 FP for Disengage + Dodge.
  - Step of the Wind: Dash as BA; 1 FP for Disengage + Dash, jump doubled.
- **Uncanny Metabolism** (1/Long Rest): on initiative, regain all FP and 1d6+3 HP.
- **Deflect Attacks** (Reaction): cut B/P/S attack damage by 1d10+7; at 0, 1 FP to
  redirect 2d6+4 (DEX save DC 12).
- **Open Hand Technique:** a Flurry hit can Addle (no opportunity attacks), Push (STR
  save or 4.5 m away) or Topple (DEX save or Prone).
- **Human:** Resourceful (Heroic Inspiration on a Long Rest), Skillful (Perception),
  Versatile (Skilled: Survival, Medicine, Athletics).
- **Criminal:** Alert.
