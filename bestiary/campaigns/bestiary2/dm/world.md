# bestiary2 · the world (short)

Late autumn. **Brack**, a palisade town of ~400 at a river ford, where the farms end and
the Greywood hills begin. Safe; a temple, an inn, a market every fifth day.

## People in town (by job, one want each)

- **Hanna the reeve**: runs Brack. Wants the roads and pastures safe before winter. Pays
  bounties from the town chest.
- **Ostrik the innkeeper** (the Ford Inn): rooms 5 SP/night, meals 3 SP. Wants paying
  guests; trades in rumours.
- **Bel the shepherd**: lost 14 sheep and her dog this month. Wants the thing dead.
- **Gerd the trapper**: head of the Hunters' Lodge. Wants furs and the north woods open.
- **Brother Tamm the temple keeper**: sells Potions of Healing (50 GP, has 3). Wants the
  old shrine at Stonefield cleansed and reconsecrated.

## Factions

- **The Reeve's Watch**: Hanna and a dozen guards; want Brack safe and the tolls paid.
- **The Hunters' Lodge**: trappers led by Gerd; want furs and no tolls on the north road.
- **The Copper Hill miners**: out-of-work miners; want the old mine reopened.
