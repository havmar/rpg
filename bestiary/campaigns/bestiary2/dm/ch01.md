# ch01 prep · Frost Hollow

**Omen 1:** Winter Wolf (CR 3, 700 XP), as a place: its den.

- **The problem:** a winter wolf has denned in Frost Hollow, a north hill hollow where
  frost lies all day. It takes Bel's sheep at night. It speaks Common and Giant.
- **The job:** Hanna the reeve pays **100 GP** for its head. Gerd the trapper offers
  **150 GP** for the white pelt unmarked by fire, and the Lodge's goodwill; Hanna wants
  the head shown at the gate. (One choice: who gets the kill.)
- **The den:** a hollow 30 m across, a cave mouth at the north end, ice on the ground
  (difficult terrain near the cave). The wolf (75 HP) and **one wolf** it has bullied
  into its pack (11 HP). 750 XP: High for two. Two more wolves if they dawdle (not
  more).
- **The wolf's mind:** smart, cruel, lazy. Would talk: leave it be, and it eats Brack's
  sheep, not Brack's people... until winter. Can be bribed with meat for a while.
- **Has:** in the cave, the bones of Joss the trapper (Gerd's missing man) and his pack:
  32 GP, a silver ring (25 GP), and one item by `loot.py --rarity Uncommon`.
