# bestiary2 · rules for this campaign

## Rolled at the new game (Day 0)

- **Classes, first try:** d12 each gave Cleric (Life) and Monk (Open Hand), both Front.
  The human then fixed the rule (one from each list) and the classes were rerolled.
- **List:** d2 = 2 → the PC takes the Back list, the companion the Front.
- **PC class:** d6 = 1 → Back 1: **Wizard (Evoker)**.
- **Companion class:** d6 = 2 → Front 2: **Fighter (Champion)**.
- **Scores:** rolled, 4d6 keep the highest 3, six times each (the DM's choice).
  - PC: 9, 9, 15, 11, 14, 11 (modifiers total +2).
  - Companion: 16, 6, 15, 13, 8, 8 (modifiers total +2).
- **Starting magic item** (SRD, Starting at Higher Levels: 1 Common at level 2–4), by
  `loot.py --rarity Common`: PC, Bead of Nourishment (kept through the reroll); companion, Potion of Climbing.

## Rulings

- **Weak set reroll:** a set of rolled scores whose modifiers total less than +1 is
  rerolled whole. (SRD silent; carried over from bestiary1 as a table rule.)
- **Duplicate proficiency** from a background: take another of the same kind instead.
- **Units:** metres and kilograms throughout (SRD feet ÷ 5 × 1.5 for squares; lb × 0.45).
