# Hilde Ashgrove's journal

**Day 1.** I reached Brack with my spellbook, a staff and forty-three gold; the temple shelves had nothing left to teach me, and the Greywood ruins might.
I need coin to copy what I find there, and I'm told I'll be travelling with a monk I haven't met yet.

**Day 1, night, the Ford Inn.** Killed the white wolf of Frost Hollow (15 km north, a bowl of ice with a cave) and its grey dog. It talked: wanted the sheep and the town's flocks. We baited it with an old ewe from Bel the shepherd, then Web, then Sleep. Oda's greatsword while it slept, Magic Missiles as it ran. I took a 21-point breath of cold; it froze us both, and it's a beast that thinks and lies back. Lessons: Sleep works on big things; Fire would have been faster but Magic Missile kept the pelt.
- **People.** Oda Fenn, sellsword: hard, careful, few words, honest about splits; she leads through doors and I stay behind her. I trust her. Hanna the reeve paid 100 GP for the head and says she'll have more jobs. Gerd the trapper, head of the Hunters' Lodge, paid 120 for the torn pelt (150 if clean). We brought his man Joss's bones home; the Lodge is friendly to me now. Bel the shepherd lost 14 sheep and her dog. Ostrik keeps the inn.
- **What I have.** 150 GP 7 SP; my owl familiar. A bronze ring with a green stone from Joss's pack: warm in the cold, Transmutation aura. Gerd gave it to me; Joss probably found it near the Hollow.
- **Next.** Study the ring over a Short Rest to learn what it does. See Hanna the reeve for work. Ask about the Greywood ruins and any spellbooks or scrolls for sale; copying costs 50 GP a level, so save for that.
- **Worry.** My HP is thin (23). Where did Joss's ring come from, and is there more up there?
