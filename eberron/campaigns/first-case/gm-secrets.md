# GM secrets: first-case ("The Making Key")

GM eyes only. Simple pulp-noir one-shot, 3–4 scenes, Sharn, Zarantyr (late winter), 998 YK.

## The truth in one paragraph

Corin Marrow, a Cyran ex-army sapper and refugee, ran an illegal salvage trip into the
Mournland two months ago. In the ruins of the Cannith forge-city of Making he found the
**Making Key**: a 30 cm rod of dark iron and a red Siberys-dragonshard core, a Cannith
command device built to keep Cyran-forged warforged in line. He brought it to Sharn to sell,
asked around too loudly, and drew the attention of **Quill**, a changeling fixer. Corin got
cold feet, locked the rod in a **House Kundarak deposit box** (Box 77, Kundarak branch in the
Bazaar of Middle Dura), named his sister **Vessa** as the only other keyholder, and mailed her
the key. Three nights ago Quill's hired Daask muscle grabbed Corin. Corin won't talk, but
Quill found the Kundarak receipt in his coat and knows the key went to Vessa. Quill needs
the key *and* a good look at Vessa so he can walk into Kundarak wearing her face.

## The Making Key (the MacGuffin)

- Rod, 3 charges. Command word is Cyran ("Halt, in the name of the Queen" in old Cyran;
  Corin knows it, and it's etched in tiny script on the grip; DC 13 Int/History to read).
- Spend a charge, speak a one-word order: every warforged **built in a Cyran forge** within
  9 m must obey for one round (DC 13 Wis save to resist).
- **Ledger was built in a Brelish-contract Cannith forge: it is NOT affected.** It feels a
  ringing hum in its chest plate, like a tuning fork, whenever the rod is near or used. Use
  this for flavour and a creepy moment. Never take control of Ledger.
- Detect Magic: strong enchantment. Identify: reveals all of the above except the command word.
- Value: Cannith East pays 150 gp, no questions. Boromars/Daask would pay more. The Lord of
  Blades' agents would kill for it.

## NPCs

- **Vessa Marrow** (client). Cyran human, 28, laundry worker in Lower Dura, sharp-tongued,
  scared, proud. Lives in a refugee tenement in Malleon's Gate. Has 15 gp and her late
  father's Cyran service medal to offer. Knows: Corin went missing 3 nights ago; key came by
  Orien post 4 days ago with note "Vess—don't open it, don't give it to anyone, I'll explain.
  C."; a "polite grey-eyed man" in a good coat offered her 50 gp yesterday for "anything
  Corin left"; the Watch (Sergeant Dallow) told her Corin "probably skipped town."
- **Quill** (villain). Changeling fixer. Usual face: "Mr. Grey", a polite, forgettable man,
  grey eyes, grey coat, silver cane. Also impersonated Sergeant Dallow when Vessa visited the
  Watch post (the real Dallow never met her). Greedy, not a fanatic; plans to sell the rod to
  the highest bidder. Surrenders or flees when cornered (will try to change face and slip
  away). HP 18, AC 14, sword-cane +5 (1d8+3), hand crossbow +5 (1d6+3). Tell: absently taps
  two fingers together when lying, in every face.
- **Rask** Daask gnoll enforcer. HP 11, AC 13, spear +4 (1d8+2). Not loyal; talks if beaten
  or bribed (10 gp). Knows "the face-man" pays him and that Corin is held at the old
  Redcinder Foundry in the Cogs, near the Ashblack furnaces.
- **Kobold cutters (2–4)** Daask. HP 5, AC 12, knife +4 (1d4+2). Cowardly; flee at half
  numbers.
- **Anvil** Cyran-forged warforged mercenary Quill hired for the climax. HP 20, AC 15, great
  maul +5 (1d10+3). Tired and quiet; fought for Cyra; misses his unit. Vulnerable to the
  Making Key, and also can be talked down (DC 13 Cha/persuasion with a Cyran or soldier
  angle, advantage if Corin or Ledger speaks to him as a soldier).
- **Corin Marrow.** Cyran human, 31, beaten, chained in the foundry. Stubborn, guilty about
  dragging his sister in. Knows the command word.

## Scene plan

1. **The office (hook + action).** Vessa arrives with the key and her story. After a few
   exchanges (or when it gets slow), Rask and two kobolds come up the noodle-shop stairs to
   take the key and Vessa (Quill wants her alive to study her face). Quick fight or escape.
   A captured/bribed thug points to Redcinder Foundry and "the face-man".
2. **Legwork (pick any, don't force).**
   - *Kundarak branch, Bazaar of Middle Dura:* Vessa + key opens Box 77. Inside: the rod in
     oilcloth and Corin's note: "If you're reading this, sell it to Cannith and get out of
     Sharn." Ledger's chest hums. A grey-coated man is watching from across the hall (Quill,
     Perception DC 13 notices him tapping two fingers).
   - *Corin's room, Malleon's Gate tenement:* tossed already. Clues: hidden under floorboard,
     a hand-drawn map of a route into the Mournland marked "Making"; red cinder grit on the
     doorframe (Redcinder Foundry's slag is famously red; DC 13 Investigation/History);
     neighbour saw a gnoll and "a Watch sergeant" take Corin.
   - *Watch post, Lower Dura:* the real Sergeant Dallow is a fat, bored dwarf, nothing like
     the human "Dallow" Vessa met. That reveals a changeling.
3. **Redcinder Foundry, the Cogs (confrontation).** Hot, red-lit, chains, cooling slag
   channels, gantries. Quill (as Mr. Grey) offers a trade: Corin for the rod. Corin hangs in
   chains over a slag channel on a crane. Hidden: kobolds on the gantry, Anvil in the shadows,
   Rask if he's still on payroll. Quill double-crosses as soon as he has the rod. Pulp
   options: use the rod on Anvil, talk Anvil down, swing on the crane, Grease the gantry.
4. **Aftermath.** Vessa pays what she can (15 gp + medal, or more if they're generous). The
   rod: Cannith East pays 150 gp; Ledger may want it destroyed (smash the shard, Arcana DC
   13 to do safely); the Watch is useless. If Quill escaped he becomes a recurring villain:
   he now knows Arden's face.

## Optional twist

If the PCs leave Vessa alone at any point, Quill may take her place (real Vessa tied up in
her tenement). Tells: forgets Corin's nickname for her ("Vess"), taps two fingers when lying.

## State tracking

- Scene 2: the PCs skipped Kundarak and are going straight to Redcinder with Vessa AND the key
  (rod still in Box 77). Quill was warned by the escaped kobold. Adapted climax: Quill (as Mr. Grey)
  offers Corin in exchange for the key plus Vessa walking into Kundarak with him in the morning.
  He plans to double-cross: keep both siblings, wear Vessa's face at the bank. Without the rod,
  Anvil has to be fought or talked down (Corin may still know the command word, which is useful
  later). Defenders at Redcinder: Quill, Anvil, kobold B plus 1 more kobold on the gantry (Rask is
  out of play, tied up in Lower Dura).
  Outside: a lit window on the top floor office, one kobold lookout on the gate (sleepy), a
  side culvert where the slag channel runs out (Stealth DC 13, leads to the casting floor).
- Scene 3 (climax) begun: the PCs were heard in the culvert and Quill is ready. Positions: Quill (as Mr. Grey)
  on the gantry 6 m up, beside the crane lever. Corin chained, hanging from the crane hook 3 m over a
  cooling slag channel (hot, not molten: a drop means 2d6 burns and he's stuck). Anvil on the casting
  floor, 8 m from the culvert mouth. 2 kobolds with hand crossbows (+4, 1d6) on the far gantry.
  Offer: hand the key up and Vessa comes with Quill to Kundarak at dawn; Corin goes free. (A lie:
  he taps two fingers.)
- Rod: in Kundarak Box 77. Corin: alive, Redcinder Foundry.
