## Session 1 (2026-10-23)

### Opening scene

**GM:**

Rain has been coming down on Lower Dura for three days, sliding off the towers far above and landing on your window as soot-grey streaks. The office over Mother Pell's Noodle House smells of broth and wet wool. Ledger sits at the side table under the everbright lantern, stamping last month's numbers into the account book. They don't look good. It's a little past the eleventh bell when someone comes up the stairs fast and light. A shape stops behind the frosted glass with "A. KELL, INQUISITIVE" painted on it, and then the door opens without a knock.

The woman is in her late twenties, soaked to the skin, a laundry-worker's shawl over her red-raw hands. She has the flat vowels of someone from Cyra, a country that doesn't exist anymore. She doesn't sit. She crosses to your desk and puts something down on the blotter: a heavy brass key with a stamped steel tag hanging from it, marked with the angular dwarven runes of House Kundarak. "My name's Vessa Marrow. My brother Corin's been gone three nights. The Watch says he skipped town." Her jaw tightens. "Corin doesn't skip. He mailed me this four days ago with a note telling me not to open it and not to give it to anyone. And yesterday a very polite man in a grey coat offered me fifty gold for anything Corin left behind." She glances back at the door, then at you. "I've got fifteen gold. Will you take the case, Mister Kell?"

What do you do?

**Ledger (AI):** looks up from the account book and quietly stamps a line: "Fifteen gold. Received: 0."
