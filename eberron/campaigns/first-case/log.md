# Campaign log: first-case

**System:** Eberron (Eberron Lite house rules). **Setup:** 1 (subagent GM; the main session
plays Ledger, the human plays Arden Kell). **Format:** simple one-shot, 3–4 scenes, pulp noir
in Sharn.

## Scene 1: the office above Mother Pell's
Vessa Marrow, a Cyran refugee, hired Arden (15 gp, deferred) to find her missing brother Corin. She
brought a House Kundarak deposit key (Box 77, Bazaar of Middle Dura) that Corin mailed her. Daask
thugs (a gnoll and two kobolds) broke in for the key and for Vessa "alive". Arden shot the gnoll and
Ledger knocked it out; one kobold escaped. The captured kobold Snik said the "face-man" (grey coat,
grey eyes, "many faces") hired them and Corin is held at "the red place", which Ledger identified as
Redcinder Foundry in the Cogs. The gnoll was tied up and dumped in the street, Snik was released,
and Arden owes Pell 5 gp for the door. Ledger is at 11/12 HP.
