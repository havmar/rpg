# World: established facts (players know these)

- **When:** Zarantyr (late winter), 998 YK, four years after the Treaty of Thronehold.
- **Arden's office:** one room above **Mother Pell's Noodle House** on a rain-slick street in
  Lower Dura, Sharn. Frosted-glass door with "A. KELL, INQUISITIVE" painted on it, a desk, two
  chairs, a filing cabinet, a window over the street. Noodle steam comes up through the
  floorboards.
- **Vessa Marrow:** client, a Cyran refugee woman who came to the office with a brass key.
- **Corin Marrow:** Vessa's brother, 31, ex-Cyran army sapper, now does salvage and dock work.
  Room in a refugee tenement on Lantern Row, Malleon's Gate. Missing three nights.
- **The key:** House Kundarak deposit key, **Box 77, Kundarak branch in the Bazaar of Middle
  Dura**. Kundarak boxes open only for a named keyholder.
- **Corin's note:** "Vess. Don't open it, don't give it to anyone. I'll explain. C."
- **The grey man:** a polite man in a grey coat with a silver-topped cane offered Vessa 50 gp for
  anything Corin left.
- **Sergeant Dallow** of the Lower Dura Watch told Vessa that Corin "probably skipped town".
