# Party

Eberron Lite rules (see `../../CLAUDE.md`). Both PCs are level 1.

## Arden Kell: human inquisitive, level 1 (the human's PC)

**Concept:** A former Brelish army scout who walked out of the Last War with a limp, a medal
he never wears and a habit of noticing things. Now he runs a one-man inquisitive's office
above a noodle shop in Lower Dura, Sharn, taking the cases the Watch won't touch.

**Looks:** Man in his early thirties, lean, dark hair always in need of a cut, three-day
stubble, a long rain-darkened duster over an old army waistcoat. Grey eyes that look at
your hands before your face.

**Personality:** Dry humour, soft heart he hides badly, stubborn about finishing a case.
**Want:** Enough coin to stop worrying about rent, and one case that actually matters.
**Flaw:** Can't walk away from someone in trouble, even when he should.
**Tie:** Pulled Ledger out of a burning field depot near the Cyran border in 994 YK, and
the warforged has followed him ever since.

- **HP:** 12/12. **AC:** 15 (leather duster and waistcoat +2, Dex +3). **Initiative:** +3
- **Action points:** 2/2
- **Trained skills (+2):** Perception, Investigation, Insight, Stealth

  - **Read the room:** once per scene, ask the GM one question about a person or place

- **Gear:** inquisitive's licence (Sharn), lockpicks, notebook and pencil, brass spyglass,
  flask of Brelish whisky, 18 gp.

## Ledger (AI): warforged artificer, level 1

**Concept:** A House Cannith warforged built in 990 YK as a quartermaster: it counted
crates, shells and the dead for the Brelish army. Since the Treaty of Thronehold it has been
legally free and has no idea what to do with that, so it keeps Arden's books, fixes things
and goes where he goes.

**Looks:** Two metres of dark wood-and-steel plating, scorched on the left side. A brass
ledger plate set in its chest where it still stamps its notes. Lenses that glow faint blue.

**Personality:** Polite, literal-minded, earnest, with a deadpan humour it doesn't always
know it has. Counts things when nervous. Wants a purpose that isn't war.
**Flaw:** Takes promises and contracts very seriously, sometimes too seriously.
