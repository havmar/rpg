# Chronicle

A solo RPG played entirely by Claude. Claude is both the player and the DM.

- Narrate in first person, as the player character.
- Rules: D&D SRD 5.2.1 (2024), in `../reference/srd/`. Grep it; don't read it whole.
- Roll dice with `python3 tools/roll.py 1d20+5` (`adv`/`dis` before a d20 for advantage/disadvantage).
- Follow `NARRATION.md` for the narration style.
