# Nine Bridges

N Division's ground: nine precincts under one mast. The station stands under the mast in the centre. A leg is one precinct's crossing, about an hour by tram and on foot; the encounter die is rolled once a leg.

## The Core (under the mast: grades 1 to 3)

- **Vinegar Yard** (Centre, the station is here): a brewery everyone smells before they see.

## The Fringe (the mast's edge: grades 4 to 7)

- **Lime Wharf** (N, 1 leg from the station): an infirmary, a workhouse and a long queue.
- **Clock Street** (W, 1 leg from the station): rows of back-to-back tenements, full.
- **Gasworks** (E, 1 leg from the station): a cemetery, full, with a waiting list.
- **Printers' Row** (S, 1 leg from the station): a hospital run by a nursing order.

## The Static (the corners, where no mast reaches: grades 8 to 10)

- **Chapel Fields** (NW, 2 legs from the station): a canal basin crowded with barges.
- **Ropewalk** (NE, 2 legs from the station): a slaughterhouse and the tanneries downwind.
- **Tallow Row** (SW, 2 legs from the station): a music hall and the streets that live off it.
- **Needle Street** (SE, 2 legs from the station): a covered market that never quite closes.

## People of the station

- **The Director, Harold Crane** (human): thirty years on the Board, never a dungeoneer; wants his ledger short and his Static corners quiet, in that order.
- **The Reader, Dr Edith Lark** (gnome): sits under the ears with a slide rule and cold tea; wants a month of readings nobody argues with.
- **The ledger clerk, Walter Finch** (human): reads the ledger out at dawn in a chapel voice and runs the exchange; wants every officer to call in, and to keep it short.
- **The fitter, Meg Stubbs** (orc): rebuilds automatons in the cellar shop and talks to them more than to people; wants cores brought home, whatever state the rest is in.
- **The auditor, Mr Ernest Vale** (tiefling): the Board's man, counts breaches closed against wages paid; wants to know why Probationer Pike walked out, and why he was let back.
- **The wall**: by the stair, seat, name and day. The last Owl on it is Mary Dunn, day -31.
