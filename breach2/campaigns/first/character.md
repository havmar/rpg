# The Owl · grade 1 · N Division, Nine Bridges

Pair's purse: **30 GP** (Fletcher 21 GP, Rookwood 9 GP). XP: **350** (level 3 at 450). Relics: the Cloak of the Manta Ray from #19, attuned to Fletcher on day 47. (The copper scale was Ives's, and went dead with his bond on day 41.)

## Probationer Bob Fletcher (he/him)

Orc Soldier · Ranger 2 (Hunter at level 3) · Front · transferred onto the seat on day 47.
Wants out, and is counting the days to the next review.

- **HP** 18 (d10) · **AC** 15 (Studded Leather 12 + DEX 3) · **Speed** 9 m · **Initiative** +3 · **Proficiency** +2
- **STR** 10 (+0) · **DEX** 16 (+3) · **CON** 12 (+1) · **INT** 9 (−1) · **WIS** 14 (+2) · **CHA** 8 (−1)
  (rolled 10/14/11/9/14/8; Soldier gives DEX +2, CON +1)
- **Saves:** STR +2, DEX +5. **Passive Perception** 14.
- **Skills:** Stealth +7 (Expertise, Deft Explorer), Perception +4, Survival +4 (class); Athletics +2, Intimidation +1 (Soldier).
- **Tools:** Dice. **Languages:** Common, Orc, Goblin, Draconic, Sylvan.
- **Orc:** Darkvision 36 m. **Adrenaline Rush** 2 per Short or Long Rest: Dash as a Bonus Action and gain 2 Temporary HP. **Relentless Endurance** once per Long Rest: dropped to 0 HP but not killed outright, he is at 1 HP instead.
- **Savage Attacker** (Soldier): once a turn on a weapon hit, roll the damage dice twice, keep either.
- **Fighting Style: Archery:** +2 to hit with ranged weapons (counted below).
- **Weapon Mastery:** Longbow (Slow: a hit takes 3 m off the target's Speed until his next turn), Scimitar (Nick: the extra Light-weapon attack is part of the Attack action).
- **Favored Enemy:** *Hunter's Mark* always prepared, 2 free castings per Long Rest (Bonus Action, Concentration, 1 hour: +1d6 Force on each weapon hit against the marked creature).
- **Spellcasting**, Wisdom: spell attack +4, save DC 12. **Slots:** 2 × level 1.
  Prepared: *Cure Wounds* (2d8+2 HP, touch), *Ensnaring Strike*, *Goodberry* (ten berries, 1 HP each, good for a day).

**Attacks**
- Longbow: +7 to hit, 1d8+3 Piercing, 45/180 m, Slow. Quiet.
- Scimitar: +5 to hit, 1d6+3 Slashing; then the Shortsword in the other hand as part of the same action: +5 to hit, 1d6 Piercing.
- Spear: +2 to hit, 1d6 Piercing (1d8 two-handed), thrown 6/18 m.

**Gear:** Studded Leather Armor, Longbow, 40 Arrows, 2 Quivers, Scimitar, Shortsword, Spear, Shortbow, Pike's battleaxe (from Bess Harrow), Cloak of the Manta Ray (attuned: breathes underwater, swims 18 m), sprig of mistletoe (focus), Explorer's Pack (backpack, bedroll, 2 flasks of oil, 10 days' rations, rope, tinderbox, 10 torches, waterskin), Healer's Kit (10 uses: stabilise a dying creature without a check), dice, traveler's clothes. 21 GP.

## Rookwood (he/him)

Automaton · Druid 2 (Land at level 3) · Back · built for Fletcher and bound to him on day 47. He knows nothing of the seat before that morning.

- **HP** 17 (d8) · **AC** 15 (Leather 11 + DEX 1 + Integrated Protection 1 + Shield 2) · **Speed** 9 m · **Initiative** +1 · **Proficiency** +2
- **STR** 11 (+0) · **DEX** 13 (+1) · **CON** 14 (+2) · **INT** 12 (+1) · **WIS** 16 (+3) · **CHA** 11 (+0)
  (an automaton has no background: no score increases, no origin feat)
- **Saves:** INT +3, WIS +5. **Passive Perception** 15.
- **Skills:** Perception +5, Medicine +5 (class); Persuasion +2 (protocol); Arcana +4, Nature +4 (Magician adds WIS).
- **Tools:** Herbalism Kit, Tinker's Tools. **Languages:** Common, Sylvan, Druidic.
- **Automaton:** Advantage on saves against Poisoned, Resistance to Poison, no food, drink, air
  or sleep, can't be put to sleep by magic; Long Rest is 6 hours motionless and aware; Bound;
  **Gizmo** at a breach's anchor (carries the 25 kg case; 10 minutes in peace to set up and run, does nothing else meanwhile).
- **Primal Order: Magician** (one extra cantrip; WIS added to Arcana and Nature).
- **Wild Shape** 2 per Long Rest (one back on a Short Rest): Bonus Action, a known Beast form of CR 1/4 or less with no Fly Speed, for 1 hour, 2 Temporary HP; no spells while in it. Known forms: Rat, Riding Horse, Spider, Wolf.
- **Wild Companion:** a Magic action and a spell slot or a Wild Shape use casts *Find Familiar*.
- **Spellcasting**, Wisdom: spell attack +5, save DC 13. **Slots:** 3 × level 1.
  Cantrips: *Guidance*, *Produce Flame* (a flame in the hand as a Bonus Action; hurled 18 m as a Magic action, +5 to hit, 1d8 Fire), *Spare the Dying* (4.5 m: a creature at 0 HP is Stable).
  Prepared: *Entangle*, *Faerie Fire*, *Fog Cloud*, *Healing Word*, *Cure Wounds*; *Speak with Animals* always.

**Attacks**
- Sickle: +2 to hit, 1d4 Slashing. Quarterstaff (his focus): +2 to hit, 1d6 Bludgeoning.

**Gear:** Leather Armor (part of the body), Shield, Sickle, Quarterstaff, Herbalism Kit, Explorer's Pack (backpack, bedroll, 2 flasks of oil, 10 days' rations, rope, tinderbox, 10 torches, waterskin), the gizmo case (25 kg), the call box (5 kg). 9 GP.
