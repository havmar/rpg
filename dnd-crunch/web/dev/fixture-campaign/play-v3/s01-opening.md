The tower stands on the level-1 mountains at B5, Hermit (Renown 0), Hold 3 of 3, no sites and no goal yet. Six realms hold the other nineteen hexes, each working on one goal with a clock; the world's turn at the end of the month rolls a d6 for each.

- **Next door:** the Gnomes (Mayor Fizwick, bad: robs its neighbours) in A5 with their Garrison, the Orcs (Chief Vorga, bad: takes people for soldiers) in B4 at their seat, and the Orcs again in C5.
- **Friendly:** the Humans and the Elves (both good: fights tyrants) start at standing +1; an *ally* goal is 3 marks and a Contact there is DC 10.
- **Rumours:** the Gnomes and the Tieflings both want to raid the Elves (clocks of 2); the Orcs and the Goblinkind both mean to press the Humans (clocks of 3).
