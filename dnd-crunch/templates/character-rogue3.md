# Morten — Rogue 3 (Thief) + Book 3

Human (Medium), male (he/him). Background: Criminal. XP 900 (level 4 at 2,700).
Two party members in one body (`hotel.md` section 11): the **Class** is a Rogue, the
**Book** is a wizard's spellbook cast by the roll.

| Attack | To hit | Damage | Range | Mastery |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Oil flask (thrown, replaces an attack) | DEX save DC 13 | covered: +5 fire if hit by fire within 1 min | 6 m | — |

- **Vex** (Shortsword, Shortbow): on a hit, he has Advantage on his next attack roll
  against that creature before the end of his next turn. That Advantage turns on Sneak
  Attack.
- **Light:** after an Attack with the Shortsword, a Bonus Action attack with a dagger in
  the other hand, no DEX on its damage.

### Features

- **Sneak Attack 2d6:** once per turn, extra damage on a hit with a Finesse or Ranged
  weapon if he has Advantage, or if an ally who isn't Incapacitated is within 1.5 m of the
  target and he doesn't have Disadvantage. A familiar is an ally. Not with spells.
- **Cunning Action:** Dash, Disengage or Hide as a Bonus Action.
- **Steady Aim:** Bonus Action, Advantage on his next attack this turn; only if he hasn't
  moved, and his Speed is 0 for the rest of the turn.
- **Expertise:** Stealth and Sleight of Hand.
- **Thieves' Cant**, and Abyssal.
- **Weapon Mastery:** Shortsword and Shortbow (changeable after a Long Rest).
- **Fast Hands (Thief):** as a Bonus Action, a Sleight of Hand check (pick a lock, disarm
  a trap, pick a pocket), the Utilize action, or the Magic action to use a magic item.
- **Second-Story Work (Thief):** Climb Speed 9 m; jump distance by DEX.
- **Alert** (Criminal origin feat): +PB to Initiative; may swap Initiative with a willing
  ally.
- **Magic Initiate (Wizard)** (Human origin feat, INT): Light, Mage Hand, Mage Armor.
- **Human:** Resourceful (Heroic Inspiration after each Long Rest: reroll any one die),
  Skillful (Arcana).

## The Book: Book 3 (roll to cast, no slots)

- **Casting check:** d20 **+4** vs DC 10 + spell level (cantrip 10, L1 11, L2 12).
  Fail = spell lost until Long Rest (no Arcane Recovery). Nat 1 = mishap. Nat 20 = double
  one numeric effect.
- **Spell save DC 12** · **Spell attack +4** (one roll = cast + attack).
- **Highest castable level:** 2 · **Focus:** the wand (Arcane Focus) or the spellbook.
- **Hands:** a Somatic component needs a hand, a Material one the focus held. Shortsword
  and wand cast anything; the bow holds in one hand between shots.
- **Rituals** need the spell prepared (no Ritual Adept), then no check, +10 minutes.
- **Magic Initiate** spells are cast by the roll too; **Mage Armor once per Long Rest
  with no check.**
