**Room 102 · Axe Beak (CR 1/4) · 50 XP · searched, no brick**
- **The door:** a 5 GP gift moved the beak from wary to "ignores him", so he searched the room around it.
- **Cheese:** a Detect Magic Ritual showed an aura in the beak's suitcase. Morten cast Guidance on himself, then lifted the item out with Mage Hand. Sleight of Hand rolled 15 +1 +2 = 18 against the beak's passive Perception 10, so the beak never noticed. It was a pair of crystal lenses.
- **Noise check:** a 2, so housekeeping came. He paid the 10 GP damages on the spot to keep the Staff at 0.

**Room 103 · Giant Lizard (CR 1/4) · 50 XP · killed, no brick**
- **The door:** reaction 5, so it would attack if approached.
- **How it went:** Sleep from the corridor, and it failed both saves (6, then 7). He stepped into the doorway and fired Scorching Ray point-blank. An Unconscious target within 1.5 m takes a critical hit, so the first ray did 4d6 = 20 fire to a 19 HP lizard. Two rays went unused.
- **Luggage:** a Spell Scroll of Charm Person, 5 GP and a tin of scale polish.

**Room 104 · Black Bear (CR 1/2) · 100 XP · killed, brick**
- **The door:** reaction 3. This time Sleep failed, because the bear saved on a 16, and it waded out of the bath to the door.
- **The dice:** its first Rend hit on 22 for 7 damage. Shield turned two other hits into misses and cost his last level 1 slot. Thunderwave from the Book held, but the bear saved, taking 5 dmg and not being pushed.
- **How it ended:** Web from the Book. The bear dodged it on a 20, then failed when its turn started inside the webs. Restrained, it missed with every Rend. Morten stepped out of reach, and three Scorching Rays with Advantage all hit for 20 fire.
- **Result:** a brick came out of the bathroom wall on a 2. Luggage was 10 GP and a rubber duck with a monocle.
- **Short Rest** in the searched room: the wandering check was quiet, and one Hit Die healed 3 HP. Holding the lenses through the rest identified them as **Eyes of Charming**: 3 charges a day, each casting Charm Person at save DC 13.

**Room 105 · Violet Fungus (CR 1/4) · 50 XP · killed, no brick**
- **The door:** reaction 1. The fungus can't work a lock, so housekeeping leaves its door on the latch.
- **How it went:** it moves only 1.5 m a turn, so Morten burned it with Fire Bolt. Hits of 10 and 5 brought it to 3 HP, then two damage rolls came up 1. It reached him and landed two Rotting Touches for 8 necrotic. He turned 2 Sorcery Points into a level 1 slot for Shield, which stopped two more touches, and a natural 20 on Fire Bolt finished it.
