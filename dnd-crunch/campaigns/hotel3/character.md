## The Class: Sorcerer 3 (Draconic), slots, no check

- **Spellcasting (CHA):** save DC **13** · spell attack **+5** · focus: the crystal.
  Spells are cast as written: from a slot, with no casting check. Cantrips need no slot.
- **Spell slots:** level 1 ●●●● · level 2 ●● (back after a Long Rest).
- **One slot a turn** (`Spells.md`): one slot-cast spell per turn; Book spells don't count.
- **Innate Sorcery** (2 a Long Rest): Bonus Action, 1 minute: Sorcerer spell save DC +1
  (14) and Advantage on Sorcerer spell attacks.
- **Font of Magic:** 3 Sorcery Points (back after a Long Rest). A slot becomes points equal
  to its level (no action); points become a slot as a Bonus Action (level 1 for 2, level 2
  for 3).
- **Metamagic** (one option a spell; Sorcerer or Book spells alike):
  - **Quickened Spell** (2 points): an Action spell cast as a Bonus Action; no other level
    1+ spell that turn, before or after.
  - **Subtle Spell** (1 point): no Verbal, Somatic or Material components (except costly or
    consumed ones). Nobody sees him cast.
- **Draconic Resilience:** +3 HP (+1 a Sorcerer level), AC 10 + DEX + CHA unarmoured.
- **Alert** (Human origin feat): +PB to Initiative; may swap Initiative with a willing ally.
- **Magic Initiate (Cleric)** (Acolyte origin feat, CHA): Guidance, Sacred Flame, Cure
  Wounds. Its spells are cast by the roll (d20 **+5**), except **Cure Wounds once per Long
  Rest with no check**, or from a Sorcerer slot with no check, as the feat says.
- **Human:** Resourceful (Heroic Inspiration after each Long Rest: reroll any one die),
  Skillful (Arcana).

## The Book: Book 3 (roll to cast, no slots)

- **Casting check:** d20 **+4** vs DC 10 + spell level (cantrip 10, L1 11, L2 12).
  Fail = spell lost until Long Rest (no Arcane Recovery). Nat 1 = mishap. Nat 20 = double
  one numeric effect.
- **Spell save DC 12** · **Spell attack +4** (one roll = cast + attack).
- **Highest castable level:** 2 · **Focus:** the wand (Arcane Focus) or the spellbook.
- **Rituals** need the spell prepared (no Ritual Adept), then no check, +10 minutes.
- Sorcerer slots never cast Book spells. Concentration is one spell at a time across both.
