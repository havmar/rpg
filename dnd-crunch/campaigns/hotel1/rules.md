# Hotel 1 · campaign rules

The Hotel, as `dnd-crunch/hotel.md` describes it, with the crunch house rules from
`dnd-crunch/CLAUDE.md`. Seed 10 (`gen_dungeon.py --seed 10`); the floors and the GM's marks
are in `gm-secrets.md`.

- **Morten** is a fresh Wizard 3 (Evoker): 900 XP, starting gear, 47 GP 7 SP 5 CP
  (`character.md`).
- **Who plays Morten:** the GM. Played in chat, in Markdown.
- **Style:** cheese everything. Table mode open. Report style: the defaults.
- **The Hotel as it was before classes and magic items.** `hotel.md` sections 11 and 12
  don't apply here: Morten stays a plain Wizard 3 (Evoker) casting every spell by the roll,
  luggage has no magic items, and the gift shop has no shelf or window. The d6 after a
  Long Rest has no lost and found on a 5, and event 10 is the old **Lost and found**: a
  random Common or Uncommon item from `reference/srd/Magic Items/` turns up at the desk,
  and the Staff sell it to the first guest who asks, 100 GP (Common) or 300 GP (Uncommon).
- **Room 101 is Morten's own room.** It has no guests and no XP. He searches it like any
  room (one hour, a noise check, a brick check), and it counts toward the rooms he has
  searched on floor 1.
