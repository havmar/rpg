## Spellcasting (house rule: roll to cast, no slots)

- **Casting check:** d20 **+6** vs DC 10 + spell level (cantrip 10, L1 11, L2 12).
  Fail = spell lost until Long Rest. Nat 1 = mishap. Nat 20 = double one numeric effect.
- **Spell save DC 14** · **Spell attack +6** (one roll = cast + attack).
- **Highest castable level:** 2 · **Focus:** quarterstaff (arcane focus) or spellbook.

## Features

- **Arcane Recovery** (house-ruled): once per Long Rest, after a Short Rest, regain lost
  spells totalling up to 2 levels (a cantrip counts as 1).
- **Ritual Adept:** cast Ritual spells from the spellbook unprepared.
- **Scholar:** Expertise in Investigation.
- **Evocation Savant / Potent Cantrip** (Evoker 3).
- **Alert** (Human origin feat): +PB to Initiative; may swap Initiative with a willing ally.
- **Magic Initiate (Wizard)** (Sage origin feat): Light, Mage Hand, Mage Armor.
- **Human:** Resourceful (Heroic Inspiration after each Long Rest: reroll any one die),
  Skillful (Perception).
