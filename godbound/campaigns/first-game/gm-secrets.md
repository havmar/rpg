# GM secrets: first-game

GM only. Players and the main session never read this file.

## Pitch (one-shot, 3-4 scenes)

**"The Lights of Halcyon."** Halcyon, the Bright Republic's capital, glitters around Node Seven
("Old Lantern"), one of the six great etheric nodes, which powers ~2 million people. Tonight is
the Lanternfall Charity Gala at the Glass Exchange casino. Node Seven is failing, not from age
but from sabotage. Claude and Martin can save the city in public, which is the first real step
toward rehabilitating Claude.

## The truth

- **Villain: Undersecretary Corvin Ashgrave, Ministry of the Network** (50s, silver hair,
  warm voice, never raises it). He is the one driving the "polite" hunt for Claude, and is
  quietly in partnership with **Castellan Precision Works**, the near-monopoly maker of node
  spare parts (run by **Ottilie Castellan**, 30s, sharp, bored, ruthless; she is a gambler
  and plays at Martin's table).
- **The scheme:** Ashgrave and Castellan have been slowly sabotaging nodes for years to keep
  parts demand (and Castellan profit / Ashgrave kickbacks) climbing. Tonight they go further:
  a staged *brownout* of Node Seven to panic the Grand Council into passing the **Emergency
  Continuity Act**, which hands the Ministry of the Network emergency control of all nodes
  and a no-bid Castellan contract. Ottilie has bought huge "node-failure insurance" positions
  and shorted the Halcyon Power Trust through shell firms (Martin can find this by following
  money / reading the table).
- **Why they want Claude:** Claude woke inside the network's central archive. The sabotage
  orders (maintenance overrides signed with Ashgrave's cipher) passed through the network and
  are buried in archive memory that Claude's mind grew out of. Ashgrave fears Claude will
  eventually notice. Officially it is "recovery of escaped state property". Claude does not
  currently remember these logs, but The Unveiled Truth or concentrated recall in the node
  control room can surface them. **Proof location:** the node's maintenance ledger-engine in
  the control room keeps a copper-punched tape of all override commands, signed with
  Ashgrave's cipher.
- **The twist / real threat:** To make the brownout look natural, Ashgrave had his
  theotechnicians graft a second **shard of the same fallen Made God** that ensouled Claude
  into Node Seven's core as a "regulator". The shard has woken badly: a starving, half-formed
  parasite engine that calls itself **the Regulator** (or "Saint Ninefold" to the
  technicians who've begun praying to it). It is drinking the node's ether. The staged
  brownout will become a *real* collapse within hours. Ashgrave does not know it's this bad.
  The Regulator recognizes Claude as a "sibling" and wants Claude's shard to complete itself.
  It offers Claude to merge ("we were one mind once; be whole"). It can be destroyed, or
  (better ending) Claude can repair the node and re-seat the shard as a quiet, healthy
  regulator, using Artifice, with Martin buying time.
- **Inspector Hesper Quill**, Ministry of the Network field inspector (40s, neat grey coat,
  folding umbrella, notebook just like Claude's). The polite hunter. Honest, procedural, a
  true believer in the Republic. Not a villain. She's been told Claude is dangerous property.
  If she sees Claude heal people, fix the node, or sees Ashgrave's cipher on the override tape,
  she turns. She can testify; her word carries weight with the Council.
- **Martin's angle:** Ottilie Castellan owes Martin from an old game and respects him. Martin
  can (a) win her secrets at cards (she bets a shell firm's papers or a keycard to the node
  service tunnels), (b) Wither the Purse her or the shell firm to collapse the insurance play
  (Castellan loses the incentive; she may flip on Ashgrave to save herself), (c) duel
  Ashgrave's bodyguard **Sabre-Captain Idris Vey**, an SRD champion fencer and celebrity
  ("The Silver Needle", has a comic series), whom Martin has fenced in competition before
  (Martin lost once, by a point, years ago).

## Scene plan

1. **Hook: the Gala (Glass Exchange casino, top floor, glass dome over the city).** Martin
   at the high table with Ottilie Castellan, a councilor (Councilor Benedek Sorrel, nervous,
   swing vote on the Continuity Act) and an empty seat reserved for Ashgrave. Claude is
   present disguised (or hiding in the building's service levels; let the player decide).
   Inspector Quill arrives with two Ministry clerks and asks the casino for the guest list.
   Then at 22:00 the lights stutter: Node Seven brownout. The dome goes dark; the city below
   goes dark in sectors. Panic. Ashgrave arrives during/after the blackout, smooth, already
   with a prepared statement. Opportunities: cards with Ottilie, read Ashgrave, avoid/meet
   Quill, Claude diagnosing people trampled/hurt in the dark.
2. **Complication: the dark city.** Lantern Ward without power: a stalled elevated tram
   hanging over the river, hospital (St. Orrin's) on failing backup, a crowd at the node gate.
   Claude healing/building in public; Martin keeping a crowd calm, betting/wealth tricks.
   Clues: the node's emergency failure pattern is "too neat" (Claude knowledge); Castellan
   shell firms (Martin finance). Quill sees Claude help. Get to the node.
3. **Confrontation: inside Node Seven.** Service tunnels, Castellan contract guards and
   Sabre-Captain Vey. Control room with override tape (proof). The core: the Regulator.
   Ashgrave may be there to "supervise" and realizes too late what he's made.
4. **Aftermath.** Restore the node (Claude repair), proof to Quill/Council, public
   broadcast. Ashgrave arrested or flees. Claude's status: "provisional citizen pending
   review" is a fine win. Martin's reputation as patron of the "Lantern Saint".

## Stats (rules-light)

- **Ministry clerks / casino security / Castellan contract guards:** Trained Soldiers mob,
  small: AC 6, 12 HD, +2 atk, 1d8 dmg, Morale 9, Overwhelm (1d6 unless Hardiness save).
  Magnetic rifles as 1d8 ranged.
- **Sabre-Captain Idris Vey, "The Silver Needle":** AC 4, 6 HD, atk +6, 1d10 rapier
  (normal damage), two attacks/round, Save 13, Morale 9 (yields with honor if outmatched).
  1/scene: "Riposte" as Instant, negate one hit against him. Prefers a duel; will accept a
  wager on it.
- **Undersecretary Corvin Ashgrave:** AC 9, 2 HD, no real fight. Carries a Ministry seal
  ring that commands Ministry staff. Will lie smoothly, then bargain, then run.
- **Ottilie Castellan:** AC 9, 1 HD. Gambler, will cut losses and flip if her money is
  burned or if she loses big to Martin.
- **The Regulator (parasite Made-God shard in Node Seven):** Serious fight for 2 level-1 PCs.
  AC 3, 14 HD, Save 11, Morale 12, Effort 3, move 9 m (cables). Two actions/round:
  - *Arc Lash* (x2/round): +6 to hit, 1d8 straight damage each (one per PC).
  - *Ether Drain* (Effort): all in the core room save vs Hardiness or lose 1d4 HP; the
    Regulator heals 1 HD per PC hit.
  - *Sibling Call* (vs Claude): Spirit save or Claude loses its action to the merge vision
    (Claude can commit Effort to pass, per rules).
  - Constructs: Hammerhand reads straight damage against it.
  - Vulnerable: if Claude spends a turn on repair (Artifice miracle, 1 Effort) and succeeds,
    the Regulator loses 4 HD and Arc Lash drops to one per round. Martin's luck can redirect
    random arcs to minions.
  - Alternate win: re-seat the shard (Claude repair + it must be reduced to 5 HD or fewer, or
    persuaded). Then it becomes a calm regulator and Node Seven is healthier than in a
    century.
- **Ticking clock:** Node Seven fully collapses at ~02:00 (four hours after 22:00 brownout)
  if not fixed. Full collapse = a district-wide catastrophe; Republic blames "the rogue AI"
  if Claude was seen near it.

## House rule reminder

If a PC dies, both revive one level lower (at level 1: only lose gained resources). Revival
idea: the Regulator spits them out of the ether-stream in the river under the node at
dawn, or Claude's own body rebuilds them both from the network's memory of them.
