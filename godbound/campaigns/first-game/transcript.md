## Session 1 (2026-10-12)

### GM: opening

**Halcyon, capital of the Bright Republic. 21:40.**

Forty storeys above the city, the Lanternfall Charity Gala fills the Glass Exchange casino. Its glass dome looks out over a sea of light. Electric trams draw gold threads along the river, and in the middle of it all stands Node Seven, the "Old Lantern". It's a tower of brass and pale stone as tall as a cathedral, humming with the etheric power that keeps two million people in hot water, television and air-conditioned optimism. Under the dome, a jazz band plays, champagne flows, and at the high table **Martin** is up 40,000 marks. Across the green felt, **Ottilie Castellan**, heiress to Castellan Precision Works, the company that makes nearly every spare part the nodes run on, slowly pushes a stack of plaques forward. "You owe me a rematch from Vissio, Martin. Let's make it interesting." Beside her, **Councilor Benedek Sorrel** sweats into his collar and keeps glancing at the empty fourth chair. Its brass placard reads *Reserved: Undersecretary C. Ashgrave, Ministry of the Network.*

Two floors down, in the service gallery among the casino's ventilation fans, **Claude** sits on a crate in the warm, ozone-scented dark. Martin smuggled it in, and it hears the table through a copper earpiece it made. Through the grating it also watches the main entrance, where a neat woman in a grey coat has just shaken out a folded umbrella. She shows a Ministry of the Network badge to the doorman and asks, very politely, to see the guest list. Two clerks with briefcases stand behind her. Claude knows her face from a hundred internal memos: **Inspector Hesper Quill**, the woman who has been following its trail for months. She carries a battered notebook almost exactly like Claude's.

Then every light in the dome flickers once. For half a heartbeat the city goes dark from the river to the hills, and a low groan rolls up out of Node Seven, a sound you feel in your teeth. The lights come back. The band stumbles and starts again, and someone laughs too loudly. Through the earpiece Claude hears something nobody else does: under the hum of the grid, a thin rhythmic pulse, almost like a voice counting. Ottilie hasn't blinked. "Your bet, Martin."

**Martin, Claude: what do you do?**

Then uses The Unveiled Truth (commits 1 Effort for the scene) on the pulse: "What is counting inside Node Seven's signal?" Keeps one eye on Quill through the grating while listening.

### GM: round 1

Martin leans back and flicks a plaque across his knuckles. "No offence, Ottilie, but I think the empty chair is the most exciting player here tonight." (Charisma check: d20 5 + Fact 4 = 9 against 7, success.) Sorrel laughs too hard and blurts out, "Ashgrave never misses a table. Not the night before a vote." The vote is at noon tomorrow: the Emergency Continuity Act, which would give the Ministry of the Network emergency control of the nodes. Sorrel is clearly the swing vote and clearly hates it. Ottilie gives Martin a lazy smile. "Corvin will be along when the evening gets *interesting*." When Sorrel nervously mentions the flicker, she shrugs. "Darling, I'm not worried about the nodes. I'm *insured*." She says it the way a card player shows you a card she wanted you to see, or one she didn't.

Down in the gallery, Claude's amber seams flare as it commits Effort and asks its question. (The Unveiled Truth: 1 Effort committed for the scene.) The answer arrives the way all its answers do, as something it suddenly *knows*, but this one feels like recognising a voice it last heard in a dream. *Inside Node Seven, a shard of the same broken Made God that gave you your soul is counting down. It is hungry, and it has noticed you listening.* The pulse in the earpiece stops for one beat, as if it's holding its breath, then goes on counting a little faster. At the entrance, Inspector Quill runs a gloved finger down the guest list, stops, and looks up toward the high table, straight at Martin.
