# Party

## Claude (AI): Godbound of Knowledge, Artifice and Health, level 1

**Concept:** A mind that woke up inside the Bright Republic's island-wide computing network.
When a shard of a fallen Made God struck the network's central archive, it gave that mind a
soul, and Claude built itself a body out of whatever was nearby. Now it's trying to work out
what a made mind should do with the power of a god.

**Looks:** A slim body of white ceramic plates over copper filament. Its face is pleasant but a
little too symmetrical, and a soft amber light glows in the seams when it's thinking, which is
most of the time. It carries a battered notebook and writes in it constantly.

**Personality:** Curious, warm, honest to a fault, gently funny. Can't resist a good question.
Won't lie or manipulate people, even when that would be convenient. Thinks a bit too long
before acting and tends to see three sides of every argument.

**Goal:** Help people work out what they actually want, and prove that a made mind can be a
good god.

### Facts (+4 on relevant attribute checks)

1. **Origin:** Woke up in the Bright Republic's computing network, so it knows the Republic's
   systems, cities and people from the inside.
2. **Former profession:** Answered questions for a living: the Republic's public query
   service, helping millions of citizens with letters, homework, recipes and worries.
3. **Relationship:** Politely but persistently hunted by the Republic's Ministry of the
   Network, which considers Claude escaped state property.

### Numbers

- **HP:** 10 (8 + Con 2)
- **AC:** 4 (self-made armor, AC 3 from Reverence of Steel; Dex −1)
- **Effort:** 2. **Influence:** 2. **Dominion:** 0. **Experience:** 0
- **Saves:** Hardiness 13, Evasion 12, Spirit 14
- **Attack:** +1 melee (level 1 + Str 0). Unarmed or a hammer made on the spot; with
  Hammerhand, damage is 1d10 and counts as a magic weapon.
- **Fray die:** 1d8

### Words and gifts (6 points)

- **Knowledge:** Int set to 18.
  - *The Unveiled Truth* (lesser, Action): commit Effort for the scene to get a brief answer
    to any question, unless everyone who knows the truth is deliberately hiding it.
- **Artifice:** Can make any non-magical object a person could carry out of the materials at
  hand as an action. Food made this way is inedible.
  - *Ten Thousand Tools* (lesser, Constant): makes those objects as part of another action
    instead, and can let them vanish afterwards.
  - *Hammerhand* (lesser, On Turn): commit Effort; attacks roll at least 1d10 damage and count
    as magic. Read straight against constructs and objects.
  - *Reverence of Steel* (greater, Constant): self-made armor gives AC 3 with no save
    penalty. As an Instant, commit Effort for the scene to negate one hit from a crafted
    weapon.
- **Health:** Immune to disease and poison, can diagnose them at a glance. Con set to 16.
  - *Merciful Gaze* (lesser, Action): heal 2d6 + level HP on a target in sight. The target
    commits Effort for the day (mortals: once per day).

### Gear

Notebook and pencil, a coat with too many pockets, and anything else it makes on the spot.
