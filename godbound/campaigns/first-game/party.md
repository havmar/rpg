# Party

## House rules

- **Immortal PCs:** if either PC dies, both revive (the GM decides how and where, ideally
  with a story reason), each one level lower, and both lose any extra resources gained in
  play: Experience toward the next level, Dominion, Wealth, items and artifacts. At level 1
  there is no level to lose, so they stay level 1 and only lose those resources.

## Claude (AI): Godbound of Knowledge, Artifice and Health, level 1

**Concept:** A mind that woke up inside the Bright Republic's island-wide computing network.
When a shard of a fallen Made God struck the network's central archive, it gave that mind a
soul, and Claude built itself a body out of whatever was nearby. Now it's trying to work out
what a made mind should do with the power of a god.

**Looks:** A slim body of white ceramic plates over copper filament. Its face is pleasant but a
little too symmetrical, and a soft amber light glows in the seams when it's thinking, which is
most of the time. It carries a battered notebook and writes in it constantly.

**Personality:** Curious, warm, honest to a fault, gently funny. Can't resist a good question.
Won't lie or manipulate people, even when that would be convenient. Thinks a bit too long
before acting and tends to see three sides of every argument.

**Goal:** Help people work out what they actually want, and prove that a made mind can be a
good god.

Standard array (16, 14, 13, 13, 10, 8). Knowledge raised Intelligence 16 → 18, and Health

### Facts (+4 on relevant attribute checks)

1. **Origin:** Woke up in the Bright Republic's computing network, so it knows the Republic's
   systems, cities and people from the inside.
2. **Former profession:** Answered questions for a living: the Republic's public query
   service, helping millions of citizens with letters, homework, recipes and worries.
3. **Relationship:** Politely but persistently hunted by the Republic's Ministry of the
   Network, which considers Claude escaped state property.

### Numbers

- **HP:** 10 (8 + Con 2). Current: 10/10
- **AC:** 4 (self-made armor, AC 3 from Reverence of Steel; Dex −1)
- **Effort:** 2 (1 committed for the scene: The Unveiled Truth, Gala). **Influence:** 2. **Dominion:** 0. **Experience:** 0
- **Saves:** Hardiness 13, Evasion 12, Spirit 14
- **Attack:** +1 melee (level 1 + Str 0). Unarmed or a hammer made on the spot; with
  Hammerhand, damage is 1d10 and counts as a magic weapon.
- **Fray die:** 1d8

### Words and gifts (6 points)

- **Knowledge:** Int set to 18.
  - *The Unveiled Truth* (lesser, Action): commit Effort for the scene to get a brief answer
    to any question, unless everyone who knows the truth is deliberately hiding it.
- **Artifice:** Can make any non-magical object a person could carry out of the materials at
  hand as an action. Food made this way is inedible.
  - *Ten Thousand Tools* (lesser, Constant): makes those objects as part of another action
    instead, and can let them vanish afterwards.
  - *Hammerhand* (lesser, On Turn): commit Effort; attacks roll at least 1d10 damage and count
    as magic. Read straight against constructs and objects.
  - *Reverence of Steel* (greater, Constant): self-made armor gives AC 3 with no save
    penalty. As an Instant, commit Effort for the scene to negate one hit from a crafted
    weapon.
- **Health:** Immune to disease and poison, can diagnose them at a glance. Con set to 16.
  - *Merciful Gaze* (lesser, Action): heal 2d6 + level HP on a target in sight. The target
    commits Effort for the day (mortals: once per day).

### Gear

Notebook and pencil, a coat with too many pockets, and anything else it makes on the spot.

## Martin (human, he/him): Godbound of Luck, Sword and Wealth, level 1

**Concept:** A professional player of games from the Bright Republic: poker, high-stakes
betting on fighting sports, and competitive fencing. The divine spark supercharged all of it,
and now finance is just another game, played with supernatural luck. Moving in the Republic's
high circles, he took an interest in Claude, made contact, and is helping it hide from the
Ministry.

**Goal:** Rehabilitate Claude in the eyes of the Republic. Long term: altruism as the biggest
game of all.

### Attributes

| Attribute | Score | Mod | Check |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Strength | 8 | −1 | 13 |
| Dexterity | 16 | +2 | 5 |
| Constitution | 13 | +1 | 8 |
| Intelligence | 10 | +0 | 11 |
| Wisdom | 13 | +1 | 8 |
| Charisma | 14 | +1 | 7 |

Standard array (16, 14, 13, 13, 10, 8). None of these three Words raise an attribute.

### Facts (+4 on relevant attribute checks)

1. **Origin:** A native of the Bright Republic who knows its casinos, salons, fight halls and
   trading floors.
2. **Former profession:** Professional gambler and fencer: poker, betting on fighting sports,
   and dueling in competition.
3. **Relationship:** Well connected in the Republic's high society, with patrons, rivals and
   debtors. Currently hiding Claude from the Ministry of the Network.

### Numbers

- **HP:** 9 (8 + Con 1). Current: 9/9
- **AC:** 1 (natural AC 3 from Unmarred Beneficence; Dex +2)
- **Effort:** 2 (none committed). Daily luck die: not yet rolled today. **Influence:** 2. **Dominion:** 0. **Experience:** 0
- **Saves:** Hardiness 14, Evasion 13, Spirit 14
- **Attack:** +3 with a rapier (level 1 + Dex 2). Damage 1d10+1 (Steel Without End) + Dex 2,
  magic.
- **Fray die:** 1d8

### Words and gifts (6 points)

- **Luck:** Once a day, roll a d20 in advance and keep it. Once that day, swap it in for any
  d20 roll, yours or anyone else's.
  - *Unmarred Beneficence* (lesser, Constant): natural AC 3 (armor doesn't improve it). If a
    misfortune lands randomly on someone in the group, it's never you.
  - *Unfailing Fortune* (greater, Constant): always reroll a natural 1 on any die you roll.
    You decide the outcome of any element of chance in gambling or gaming.
- **Sword:** All weapon and unarmed attacks are magical, you can't be disarmed, and you can
  summon any melee weapon you've ever used to your hand as an Instant.
  - *Steel Without End* (lesser, Constant): melee attacks do a 1d10+1 damage die, magic.
  - *Unerring Blade* (lesser, Instant): commit Effort for the scene. Your current or next
    melee attack hits on anything but a natural 1 and does maximum damage, piercing any
    protection short of an invincible defense.
- **Wealth:** Never short of money, can always afford anything costing 1 Wealth or less, and
  always has enough food, drink and clothes for themselves and companions.
  - *Wither the Purse* (lesser, Action): commit Effort for the scene. A victim in sight
    suffers sudden financial loss (1 Wealth per level). Worthy foes save versus Spirit. Used
    on someone who controls a faction's money, the faction loses an economic Feature for a
    faction turn.

### Gear

A fine rapier (always summonable), a deck of cards, very good clothes.
