# GM notes

Benedict does not know any of this unless it has been seen or said in play.

## Tyrants in play

- **City (C2), Lord-Captain Henrike Vorst** (Guard Captain, CR 4; 1 quest, the
  confrontation). Captain of the Watch who made herself master of the City in a "time of
  emergency" that has never ended. Oppression: the wall tax at every gate (2 SP a head in,
  1 SP out), curfew at the ninth bell, debtors and loud mouths sent to the North Quarry in
  chains, the Watch living off the markets. She keeps the Watch inside the walls: farms
  outside don't pay the wall tax, so she won't defend them against the northmen.
  - The Old Barbican: the gatehouse fort of the City's first wall, left standing in the
    middle of town when the City grew past it; now her residence, treasury and barracks.
  - Guard: the Watch (Guards, CR 1/8, everywhere); her household guard in the Old
    Barbican, sergeants (Warrior Veterans, CR 3). Lieutenant: Sergeant Corlis Dane, who runs
    the gates and the tax. Opposition: Mother Edda of the Lantern Temple, who feeds the
    families of quarry prisoners; the guild masters, who pay and hate it.
- **Arctic (C1), Grim Ulfrun Grey-Axe** (Berserker, CR 2; 1 quest, the confrontation).
  Chief of the clans by killing the last one. Attacks the City: sends bands of young
  clansmen south over the cold moors to raid the farms north of the City wall. Cruel to
  her own: the clans' sons go raiding or go hungry; those who come back empty-handed are
  turned out on the ice. Lives in the hall at Hvitmark. Guard: her own sworn axes (more
  berserkers).

## Rulers in play

- **Forest (B2), Grandfather Ashenroot** (Treant, CR 9), ruler, decent. The settlement is
  Thornmoot: houses in and under great oaks round a mossy clearing, a centaur-run lodge
  (the Long Barn, 5 SP a night), satyr musicians, sprites, druids. Ashenroot stands at the
  clearing's edge and speaks slowly, rarely.

## Quest threads

- **Hollin's mill** (done, day 1). Tom bought Brann's band off with 10 GP and a sack of
  flour; they went north over the moor. Brann now owes Tom something and knows it. Ulfrun
  will hear a story about a singing orc and a metal man; she may send someone to look.

- **Jory's song** (done, day 1). Tom lost 5 GP to Corporal Hask at dice and asked for the
  boy (Persuasion 22 against DC 13). Hask let Jory out; Benedict scratched the cell lock
  with thieves' tools so it looks picked. Story for the morning: the boy picked the lock.
  Nobody else saw. The pair is not wanted. Hask is now their man in the Watch, for gold.
- **What Hask told them about the Lord-Captain** (true): she lives in the Old Barbican with
  her household guard and rarely leaves it. On the first day of each month she rides out in
  person behind the wall-tax chest to the Barbican treasury, from the North Gate, with
  Sergeant Dane and a handful of guards: the one time she's in the street with few men round
  her. Calendar: day 1 of the campaign is the 19th of the month; the
  next first-of-month is day 13, then every 30 days (43, 73, ...). In the confrontation, this is where and when she can be caught.
  Not a quest edge by the count (CR 4 has one quest, the confrontation itself), but it
  decides where that confrontation happens.

- **The Greenwell** (ruin B2-1, level 1, corrupted, stocked day 2). An old shrine of the
  builders: a ring of standing stones round a spring, half a day north of Thornmoot, with a
  root-cellar vault beneath. Its keeper was Mossmother (Treant, CR 9), Ashenroot's sister;
  since spring the well has gone sour (a black thread in the water) and so have she and
  her keepers. Rooms: (1) the stone ring, 3 Sprites, hostile (5): they shoot at anyone who
  comes in; (2) the yew-hall above the vault, 3 Giant Owls, hostile (4): drive off, attack
  if pressed. Mossmother in the vault by the spring, wary (7): keeps her distance, holds the
  item. All corrupted: can be cured as well as killed. The cause: the builders set a black
  seal-stone in the spring to hold down something bad in the deep water; it cracked this
  spring and the black thread leaks from the crack. Cure (revised day 3, before
  it was played): the stone is a lid, not to be lifted. Pour holy water into the crack with
  a prayer and it closes (Benedict, a paladin: no check needed if he reaches it unhindered).
  Then the black thread fades within the hour and the keepers come right: sprites and owls
  at once, Mossmother by morning.
  - The thing under the stone: old, bitter, never named; the builders could not kill it.
    Not stocked. A seed for later: a hidden B2 ruin found by `search` may be its deep vault,
    and the pair may want to finish it one day, at a high level. Ashenroot
  can't go near: the black water rises into his roots. He has told the pair the old story
  of the stone, that his sister and her keepers are in there, and asked that none be
  killed if it can be helped. He gave Tom an oak leaf from his crown: shown to the keepers,
  it gives Advantage on Persuasion with them (not a new reaction roll). Award on top: High,
  plus his thanks and the Forest's friendship.
- **The Greenwell, day 3:** wolves (5, indifferent) on the way, let pass. Sprites talked
  round with the leaf (Persuasion 18 against DC 15, Advantage): room done, Low award. Owls
  talked round (15 against 15, Advantage): room done, Low award. Pair now level 2. In the
  vault: Mossmother seen (`seen` run), wary. Tom's Persuasion 26 (nat 20) against DC 13: she
  let Benedict reach the stone. He sealed the crack with holy water and a prayer. Keepers
  cured; Mossmother came back to herself and gave them the builders' frost-glass vial
  (Potion of Resistance, cold). Ruin cleared. Ashenroot: the Forest's friendship; free
  lodging in Thornmoot; he will speak for them to anyone in the wood.
- **A young green dragon** (CR 8) watches the road from the City; met day 2, wary. Lives in
  the Forest; it noticed the pair. Name unknown.

- **The white dragon of the moor** (Adult White Dragon, CR 13), met day 4, curious. Calls
  herself Hrimgala. INT 8: greedy, blunt, easily flattered, forgets slights slowly. Ulfrun's
  clans leave her a cow on the stone at Kaldbrú every new moon so she leaves Hvitmark alone;
  she despises Ulfrun ("the axe-woman who shouts"). Wants shiny things. A gift of 5 GP or
  more of shine, or a song about her, keeps her friendly; refusing rudely risks a new
  reaction roll at −2.

- **Hvitmark** (Arctic settlement): turf longhouses round a frozen mere, a palisade of
  driftwood and whale ribs, Ulfrun's hall (Grimhall) on a mound at the north end. About 300
  clansfolk, thin and quiet. Gate toll 1 SP; the Hearth bunkhouse 1 SP. Ulfrun's guard: four
  sworn axes (Berserkers, CR 2) always in or at the hall; the young men are Warrior
  Infantry. Ulfrun's law: a band that comes home empty-handed is turned out on the ice,
  with its leader's family's furs taken. The town hates it and does nothing.
  - Brann came home day 3 with 10 GP and the story told the other way round; Ulfrun took the
    gold and let his band stay. Brann is grateful and ashamed: his story was a lie.
  - Opposition: the clan elders (old Sigrid Ravnsdottir, a Priest, CR 2, keeper of the
    clans' moot-law) remember that a chief can be unmade by the moot if a champion beats her
    in a holmgang, a ring-duel, or if the moot votes her out with the axes standing aside.
    Nobody has dared. This is the shape of the confrontation (CR 2: one quest).
  - **The confrontation, as Sigrid lays it out (day 5).** Sigrid can call the moot: heralds
    to the seven clan steadings, the thanes at the moot-stone on the third day (day 8, at
    noon). At the moot anyone of the clans may name a grievance against the chief; Ulfrun
    will answer any grievance with holmgang, as she did when she took the chief's seat. A
    foreigner may stand champion for a clansman. Ketil will name Halvar's death if Benedict
    stands for him. Holmgang: in the hazel ring by the moot-stone, champion against chief,
    until one yields, dies or leaves the ring. No spell may be cast into the ring from
    outside it ("no witchcraft over the hazel"); what a fighter does with his own body and
    his own prayers is his. A blessing given before he steps in stays with him (Bardic
    Inspiration given beforehand, Heroism cast beforehand: allowed). If the champion wins,
    the moot unmakes the chief, and Sigrid names a new one by the thanes' vote (likely
    Brann's uncle, Thane Ragnvald, a fair man).
  - **Tom's part:** speak to the moot before the duel. Persuasion DC 15 (Advantage if the
    three boys stand up in front of the thanes). On a success the thanes swear the axes to
    stand aside: the fight is fair, and if Ulfrun is beaten her sworn axes yield (the edge,
    since there are no earlier quests: this is GM judgement inside the confrontation). On a
    failure, one sworn axe steps in when Ulfrun is Bloodied, and the thanes do nothing.
  - **Ulfrun's response before the moot:** she hears of it on day 6 and sends two axes to
    find "the orc and the metal man" and warn them off (talk, threats; not murder in town,
    which would turn the moot against her). She doesn't know where the boys are.
  - Benedict's odds alone: Berserker CR 2 against a level 3 paladin, a hard, close fight.
    If Benedict dies, he's rebuilt (new class) and the confrontation is lost for now; the
    holmgang can't be repeated until a new grievance is named (a month).
- **The outcasts on the ice, day 4 night:** mephits beaten (2 dead, 1 fled). Boys alive.
  Back at Hvitmark past midnight; two sworn axes watch Brann's mother's house. The Hearth's
  keeper, old Ketil, lost his own son to the ice last winter and hates Ulfrun: he would
  hide them in the Hearth's peat store if asked (the pair doesn't know this). Sigrid
  Ravnsdottir's house is by the moot-stone at the west end of town, unwatched.
- **The outcasts on the ice** (done, day 5: boys hidden by Ketil in the Hearth's peat
  store; Tom paid 1 GP for peat; Brann told by Ketil's girl at dawn). Eyvind (Brann's younger brother, 16) and
  two other boys, turned out this morning without furs. They've dug into a snow-hollow under
  a pressure ridge, two hours north of Hvitmark on the ice. Three Ice Mephits (CR 1/2 each;
  Moderate, 300 XP at level 2) have found them and circle, laughing, breathing frost on
  them, waiting for them to die; evil, reaction roll when the pair arrives. The boys have
  until about dawn tomorrow. Brann can't go: Ulfrun's axes watch his family. Bringing them
  back into town openly defies Ulfrun's law; hiding them with Brann's mother, or taking them
  south to the City or Thornmoot, doesn't. Award: High. Effect: the clans see strangers do
  what they wouldn't, and Sigrid wants to meet the orc.

## Day 7: the eve of the moot

- Long Rest night 6; day 7 in town. Gunnhild hammered Benedict a sun amulet (5 GP).
  Ketil's girl Asa found Tom a sinew string for the lute (1 SP).
- Sigrid's word: all seven thanes have answered the heralds. **Moot-peace** from dawn to
  dusk at the moot-stone: no blade drawn there outside the ring. The boys are safe at the
  moot itself; if Ulfrun is still chief at dusk, they are outlaws again and seen.
- Brann came to the peat store at dark to see Eyvind. His report (true) of how Ulfrun took
  the chief's seat: two-handed axe, takes blows to give them; when she bleeds she laughs
  and gets faster and harder to stop (Bloodied Frenzy). Eyvind wants to stand up at the
  moot; Orm is frightened but will if Eyvind does; the third boy, Kolli, says nothing.
- **Rulings for the holmgang:** Shield of Faith, Bless or Heroism cast by Benedict on
  himself before he steps in: allowed (his own prayer, given beforehand). Lay on Hands,
  Cure Wounds and Divine Smite inside the ring: his own body and prayers, allowed. Tom's
  Cutting Words from outside: witchcraft over the hazel, not allowed. Bardic Inspiration
  given before he steps in: allowed. A fighter may yield by stepping out of the ring;
  Ulfrun is proud and checks morale only when Bloodied (WIS save DC 10, with Advantage from
  Bloodied Frenzy), and on a failure she yields rather than die before the clans.
- **Benedict's plan:** boys stand up behind Tom; Tom gives him Inspiration before he steps
  in; Shield of Faith on himself before he steps in (AC 22); sword and shield, Sacred
  Weapon on his first attack; smite with the free use and both remaining slots as hits
  land, Lay on Hands when he is low; steps out of the ring if he is close to falling and
  she is not near done.

## Day 8: the moot and the holmgang (done)

- Tom's speech with the three boys standing behind him: Persuasion 24 against DC 15
  (Advantage). The thanes swore the sworn axes to stand aside; they did, and yielded.
- Holmgang, Benedict first (initiative 19 against 18). Bardic Inspiration and Shield of
  Faith before the hazel (AC 22). R1: miss 11, Inspiration +4 makes 15, hit 9 + free
  smite 11 (Ulfrun 47). Ulfrun 13 with Disadvantage, miss. R2: hit 23, 3 + smite 13
  (Ulfrun 31, Bloodied); morale 19 with Advantage, holds; her swing (Sap and Frenzy
  cancel) natural 1. R3: Benedict misses 10; her 8 with Advantage misses. R4: natural 20,
  crit 9 + smite 22 = 31: Ulfrun to 0. Benedict knocked her out rather than kill her
  (Devotion). Benedict untouched, 28/28 HP.
- Deposed (`quest` run). The moot unmade her; thanes elected Thane Ragnvald, Brann's uncle,
  chief. Ragnvald's first acts: no band turned out on the ice again; the boys go home.
  Ulfrun's chest goes back to the families whose furs she took; from it Ragnvald gave the
  champion's side a pair of sealskin boots with white goose-feather wings (Winged Boots),
  which Ulfrun took off a southern trader years ago.
- **Open:** by moot-law an unmade chief's life is the champion's to give or take. Sigrid
  asks what is to be done with Ulfrun. Benedict recommends banishment with her furs and
  her axe: out of the clans' lands by the next new moon, never to return.
- Arctic now ruled by Ragnvald (fair). The raids on the City's northern farms stop.
