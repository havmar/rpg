# The pair

Level 1 each. Proficiency bonus +2. XP 0 each (300 for level 2).

## The lord: Bertie Tanner

Gnome (Forest Gnome, Intelligence) Wizard 1, Back. Background Soldier. Small. Speed 9 m.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 9 (−1) | 9 (−1) |

Background: +2 DEX, +1 CON (Soldier: STR, DEX, CON).

- **HP** 6 (d6 + 0). Hit Dice 1d6.
- **AC** 12 (10 + DEX); 15 with *Mage Armor*.
- **Initiative** +2. Passive Perception 9.
- **Saves:** INT +4, WIS +1 (proficient). Advantage on INT, WIS and CHA saves (Gnomish Cunning).
- **Skills:** Arcana +4, Investigation +4 (Wizard); Athletics +1, Intimidation +1 (Soldier).
- **Tools:** Dice set. **Weapons:** Simple. **Armour:** none.
- **Languages:** Common, Gnomish, Dwarvish.
- **Senses:** Darkvision 18 m.
- **Feat:** Savage Attacker (once per turn, on a weapon hit, roll the damage dice twice and use either).

### Attacks
- *Fire Bolt*: +4 to hit, 36 m, 1d10 fire.
- *Ray of Frost*: +4 to hit, 18 m, 1d8 cold and −3 m speed until his next turn.
- Dagger: +4 to hit, 1d4 + 2 piercing, thrown 6/18 m (Finesse, Light, Thrown).
- Shortbow: +4 to hit, 1d6 + 2 piercing, 24/96 m.
- Quarterstaff: +1 to hit, 1d6 − 1 bludgeoning (1d8 two-handed).

### Spellcasting (INT; save DC 12, attack +4)
- **Slots:** level 1: 2. **Arcane Recovery:** 1 level of slots on a Short Rest, once per Long Rest.
- **Cantrips:** *Fire Bolt*, *Ray of Frost*, *Light*; *Minor Illusion* (Forest Gnome).
- **Forest Gnome:** *Speak with Animals* always prepared, 2 free casts per Long Rest.
- **Spellbook (6):** *Mage Armor*, *Magic Missile*, *Shield*, *Sleep*, *Thunderwave*, *Find Familiar*.
- **Prepared (4):** *Mage Armor*, *Magic Missile*, *Shield*, *Sleep*.
- **Rituals from the book:** *Find Familiar* (1 hour + 10 GP of incense and herbs; not yet cast).

Spell notes: *Magic Missile* 3 darts of 1d4 + 1 force, 36 m, never miss. *Sleep* 18 m, a 1.5 m
sphere, WIS save or Incapacitated, then Unconscious if it fails again at the end of its next
turn (concentration, 1 minute). *Shield* reaction, +5 AC until his next turn. *Thunderwave*
4.5 m cube, CON save, 2d8 thunder and pushed 3 m, half on a save. *Mage Armor* 8 hours.

### Gear
Spellbook, quarterstaff (arcane focus), 2 daggers, spear, shortbow, quiver, 20 arrows, dice
set, healer's kit (10 uses), robe, traveller's clothes, scholar's pack. **19 GP.**

## The butler: Benedict

Celestial Robot Ranger 1, Front. Background Acolyte. Medium. Speed 9 m. Always "he".

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 4 (−3) |

Background: +2 WIS, +1 INT (Acolyte: INT, WIS, CHA).

- **HP** 11 (d10 + 1). Hit Dice 1d10.
- **AC** 16 (studded leather 12 + DEX 3, +1 Integrated Protection).
- **Initiative** +3. Passive Perception 15.
- **Saves:** STR +3, DEX +5 (proficient).
- **Skills:** Perception +5, Stealth +5, Survival +5 (Ranger); Insight +5, Religion +2 (Acolyte).
- **Tools:** Calligrapher's supplies. **Weapons:** Simple and Martial. **Armour:** Light, Medium, Shields.
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Giant.
- **Celestial Robot:** advantage on saves against Poisoned, Resistance to Poison, immune to
  disease; no food, drink or air; never sleeps. Long Rest is 6 hours motionless and aware.
  Armour is part of his body.
- **Feat:** Magic Initiate (Cleric), WIS.
- **Weapon Mastery:** Longbow (Slow), Scimitar (Nick).

### Attacks
- Longbow: +5 to hit, 1d8 + 3 piercing, 45/180 m. Slow: a hit cuts the target's speed by 3 m.
- Scimitar: +5 to hit, 1d6 + 3 slashing. Nick: the off-hand shortsword attack is part of the Attack action.
- Shortsword (off hand): +5 to hit, 1d6 piercing.
- *Sacred Flame*: DEX save DC 13, 18 m, 1d8 radiant.
- *Hunter's Mark* adds 1d6 force to each of his hits on the marked target.

### Spellcasting (WIS; save DC 13, attack +5)
- **Ranger slots:** level 1: 2.
- **Favored Enemy:** *Hunter's Mark* (bonus action, concentration, 1 hour) 2 free casts per Long Rest.
- **Prepared:** *Cure Wounds* (touch, 2d8 + 3), *Ensnaring Strike* (bonus action; on a hit, STR save or Restrained, 1d6 piercing each turn).
- **Magic Initiate (Cleric):** cantrips *Guidance*, *Sacred Flame*; *Healing Word* (bonus action, 18 m, 2d4 + 3) once free per Long Rest, or with a slot.

### Gear
Studded leather (built in), scimitar, shortsword, longbow, quiver, 20 arrows, druidic focus
(sprig of mistletoe), explorer's pack, holy symbol, book of prayers, calligrapher's supplies,
parchment (10 sheets), robe. **15 GP.**
