# The pair

Level 1 each. Proficiency bonus +2. XP 0 each (300 for level 2).

## The master: Percival Whitlock

Elf (High Elf, Charisma) Sorcerer 1, Back. Background Criminal. Medium. Speed 9 m. Always a man.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 (−2) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

Background: +2 CON, +1 DEX (Criminal: DEX, CON, INT).

- **HP** 8 (d6 + 2). Hit Dice 1d6.
- **AC** 12 (10 + DEX).
- **Initiative** +4 (Alert). Passive Perception 12.
- **Saves:** CON +4, CHA +4 (proficient). Advantage against Charmed (Fey Ancestry).
- **Skills:** Deception +4, Persuasion +4 (Sorcerer); Perception +2 (Keen Senses);
  Sleight of Hand +4, Stealth +4 (Criminal).
- **Tools:** Thieves' tools. **Weapons:** Simple. **Armour:** none.
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Dwarvish.
- **Senses:** Darkvision 18 m. **Trance:** never sleeps; a Long Rest is 4 hours of meditation.
- **Feat:** Alert (proficiency bonus to Initiative; may swap Initiative with a willing ally).

### Attacks
- *Fire Bolt*: +4 to hit, 36 m, 1d10 fire.
- *Sorcerous Burst*: +4 to hit, 36 m, 1d8 of a chosen type (acid, cold, fire, lightning,
  poison, psychic, thunder); each 8 rolled adds another d8, at most 2 extra.
- *Shocking Grasp*: +4 melee spell attack, 1d8 lightning, and the target can't make
  Opportunity Attacks until its next turn.
- Dagger: +4 to hit, 1d4 + 2 piercing, thrown 6/18 m.
- Spear: +0 to hit, 1d6 − 2 piercing.

### Spellcasting (CHA; save DC 12, attack +4)
- **Slots:** level 1: 2.
- **Innate Sorcery** (bonus action, 1 minute, 2 per Long Rest): spell save DC 13 and
  Advantage on Sorcerer spell attacks.
- **Cantrips:** *Fire Bolt*, *Sorcerous Burst*, *Shocking Grasp*, *Light*; *Prestidigitation*
  (High Elf; can be swapped for a Wizard cantrip after a Long Rest).
- **Prepared (2):** *Magic Missile* (3 darts of 1d4 + 1 force, 36 m, never miss), *Shield*
  (reaction, +5 AC until his next turn, and no damage from *Magic Missile*).
- **High Elf, later:** *Detect Magic* at level 3, *Misty Step* at level 5.

### Gear
Arcane focus (crystal), spear, 4 daggers, thieves' tools, crowbar, 2 pouches, traveller's
clothes, dungeoneer's pack. **44 GP.**

## The butler: Benedict

Celestial Robot Cleric 1, Front. Background (mechanics): Sage. Medium. Speed 9 m. Always "he".
Subclass Life Domain at level 3.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 9 (−1) | 20 (+5) | 10 (+0) |

Background: +2 WIS, +1 CON (Sage: CON, INT, WIS).

- **HP** 10 (d8 + 2). Hit Dice 1d8.
- **AC** 19 (chain mail 16, shield +2, Integrated Protection +1). Stealth at Disadvantage.
- **Initiative** +0. Passive Perception 15.
- **Saves:** WIS +7, CHA +2 (proficient).
- **Skills:** Insight +7, Medicine +7 (Cleric); Arcana +1, History +1 (Sage).
- **Tools:** Calligrapher's supplies. **Weapons:** Simple and Martial (Protector).
  **Armour:** Light, Medium, Heavy, Shields.
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic, Dwarvish.
- **Celestial Robot:** advantage on saves against Poisoned, Resistance to Poison, immune to
  disease; no food, drink or air; never sleeps. A Long Rest is 6 hours motionless and aware.
  Armour is part of his body (an hour to don or doff; can't be removed against his will).
- **Divine Order:** Protector.
- **Feat:** Magic Initiate (Wizard), WIS.

### Attacks
- *Sacred Flame*: DEX save DC 15, 18 m, 1d8 radiant; no benefit from cover.
- *Fire Bolt*: +7 to hit, 36 m, 1d10 fire.
- *Guiding Bolt*: +7 to hit, 36 m, 4d6 radiant, and the next attack on the target has Advantage.
- Longsword: +3 to hit, 1d8 + 1 slashing (1d10 + 1 two-handed).

### Spellcasting (WIS; save DC 15, attack +7)
- **Slots:** level 1: 2. Holy symbol (amulet) on the shield.
- **Cantrips:** *Guidance*, *Sacred Flame*, *Spare the Dying* (4.5 m).
- **Prepared (4, changed after a Long Rest):** *Bless* (3 creatures, +1d4 to attacks and
  saves, concentration 1 minute), *Cure Wounds* (touch, 2d8 + 5), *Guiding Bolt*,
  *Healing Word* (bonus action, 18 m, 2d4 + 5).
- **Magic Initiate (Wizard):** cantrips *Fire Bolt*, *Mage Hand*; *Shield* always prepared,
  once free per Long Rest (then with a slot).

### Gear
Chain mail (built in), shield, longsword, holy symbol (amulet), explorer's pack. **45 GP.**

## Magic items

None.
