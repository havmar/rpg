# GM notes

The butler doesn't know this file. Quest counts are the GM's only.

## C2 City: the Rakshasa (CR 13), ruler

Rules as **Lord Warden Osric Vane**, a grave grey-haired man to the City, from the Wardens'
Hall. Decent rule: fair courts, a paid Watch, low tolls. Nobody in the City knows what he is.
He keeps order because a quiet city suits him; he wants nothing from the pair and watches them.

## D3 Abyss: the Quasit (CR 1), tyrant, 0/1 (the confrontation is the only quest)

Called **Nibbet** by the dretches. Holds the Abyss settlement, a pit-town of dretches (CR 1/4),
by lies and Scare, while greater demons in the hex ignore it. Cruel to its own: works dretches
until they drop. **The attack:** on the City (C2). It sends dretches up the old tannery drain
into the City's south side at night to carry off goats, dogs and, twice now, a person, for
the pit-town's "tithe". Its guard at home: 4 dretches (with it, 400 XP, Deadly at level 1;
High at level 2 is 600 for the pair).

### Thread on offer, Day 1
The Watch posts a 25 GP bounty on whatever comes up the Tannery Drain. Tonight three dretches
(CR 1/4, 150 XP, Moderate at level 1) come up for the tanner Hobb's goats. Freeform quest,
worth a High encounter (200 XP) on top. A slain or captured dretch's trail leads back down
the drain, south-east to the Abyss pit-town: the way to Nibbet.
