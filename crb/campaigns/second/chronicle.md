# Chronicle

## Day 1

Percival Whitlock is an elf, a thief by trade with a sorcerer's spark, and Benedict is his
butler. They began in the City, which Lord Warden Vane keeps in order.

The Watch had posted a bounty on whatever was coming up the old tannery drain. Hobb the
tanner had lost goats to it, and Tam, the drain-raker's son, had been taken four nights
before. The pair took the bounty and waited in Hobb's yard. At midnight dretches climbed out
of the culvert.
