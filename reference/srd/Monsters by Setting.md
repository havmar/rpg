# Monsters by Setting

The 330 SRD monsters sorted into 20 settings, so that any two monsters in one setting could
plausibly be met in the same place. Each monster is in exactly one.

Built by walking the alphabetical list: take the first monster not yet placed, gather every
remaining monster that shares its home, name the set, strike them, repeat. The settings are in
the order that produced, and each names the monster that started it.

Where a monster fits two homes it went to the one it is most at home in, not simply the earlier
one (hyenas to the savannah rather than the desert, the unicorn to the fey grove rather than the
green dragon's wood, plesiosaurs with the other dinosaurs rather than the coast).
