# State

**Where:** Selam, dead market town on the north road, the square by the well.
**When:** day 4 of the journey from Dega's Well, just after sunset. Early autumn.
**Hero:** HP 8/10, Effort 2 of 2 free, nothing committed. Shining with daylight (innate).

## Situation

Yared has just awakened as a Godbound, killing nine husks to save Saba at the well. The
refugees are crowded at the church door. Something wearing Ser Dawit Senai's face and voice
has stepped out of the barley into his light. Combat likely; it's the Stalking Horror.
