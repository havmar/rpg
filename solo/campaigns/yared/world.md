# World: GM notes (spoilers)

## Setting

Arcem, northern Ancalia, early autumn, five years after the Night Roads opened. Book entry:
`Godbound.md` line 7946. Husks: line 18582. Uncreated: line 18385.

## Places

- **Dega's Well:** hill village Yared's party left four days ago, abandoned after the well
  went black. Hollowed now.
- **Selam:** dead market town on the old north road, 60 km south of Hadra. Stone church of
  the One with a bell tower, a square well, barley gone wild in the fields around it.
- **Hadra:** Yared's home city, 60 km north of Selam. Ruined, one of the nine Night Roads
  opened in its cathedral square. Heart of the local Uncreated.
- **Kebra Ford:** walled river refuge two days south of Selam, about 3,000 souls. Ruled by
  Kassahun Soro, who calls himself High Negus. The only safe crossing of the Tekez river.
- **Asmar:** coastal enclave five days beyond Kebra Ford. Where Yared's party is going.

## People

- **The party from Dega's Well:** 38 souls. Notables: **Hani Minale**, the miller's widow
  who speaks for them (blunt, counts everyone every hour); **Saba**, her daughter, 9, who
  wanders; **old Beruk**, who was a deacon and still rings bells; **Jember**, 17, has a
  bow and too much pride.
- **Kassahun Soro**, warlord of Kebra Ford, "High Negus." Charming, tired, ruthless. Takes a
  tithe of one refugee in ten for "labour on the walls."
- **Ser Makda Alazar**, Pavian knight, one of maybe five left. Hunts the thing Kassahun deals
  with. Knew Yared as a squire and despises him for Hadra. Possible companion, if she can
  ever stand him.
- **The Gilded Company:** Patrian salvagers under **Captain Lucan Varro**, digging in Hadra's
  ruins for theotechnic relics. No interest in survivors; will pay well for a guide.

## Truths

- **The Hadra Road** is held open by a cracked celestial-engine fragment, the **Lamp of
  Hadra**, in the cathedral crypt. A Journeying miracle can seal the Road only while the
  Lamp burns; break or quench the Lamp first, or the Road reopens within a month.
- **Kassahun's tithe** goes north by night, to the **Lantern-Eater**, an Uncreated thing of
  the Hadra Road that spares Kebra Ford in return. Kassahun hates it and himself. The tithe
  wagons leave on new-moon nights; the next is in nine days.
- **The voice:** a Stalking Horror scout of the Hadra Road wears the voice of Ser Dawit
  Senai, Yared's knight, whom it ate at Hadra. It hunts Yared's party because it heard a
  Pavian squire was guiding refugees. It is the Lantern-Eater's hound.
- The Gilded Company's digging is waking the Lamp brighter; the Road is widening. Within
  two months, a Vast Mob will come down the north road.

## Foes

- **Ancalian husk** (line 18582): AC 9, HD 1, +1 gnaw 1d6, Morale 12, move 9 m, save 15+.
- **Stalking Horror, "Dawit's voice"** (line 18440ish): AC 6, HD 7, +8 x2 claws 1d12, Morale
  10, Effort 3, save 11+. Black Consumption; Cold Breath 1 (Yared must commit 1 Effort before
  committing any other while it's present). Word: Deception, perverted (it speaks in the
  voices of those it has eaten).
