# Summary

**Yared Tewolde**, 26, refugee guide and runaway Pavian squire, is leading 38 survivors of
Dega's Well south through northern Ancalia toward Kebra Ford and the coast.

## The road south (situation 1)

1. **The Well at Selam.** Benighted in the dead town of Selam, the party shelters in the
   church. Saba slips out to the well; nine husks come out of the barley. Yared awakens as
   a Godbound (Sun, Journeying, Endurance), blazing with daylight, and kills all nine. Then
   a figure with the face and voice of Ser Dawit Senai, the knight he abandoned at Hadra,
   steps out of the barley.
