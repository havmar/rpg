# Yared Tewolde

Male, 26. Ancalian: dark-skinned, lean, a road-walker's long stride, a pale scar through
the left eyebrow from the night Hadra fell. Speaks Ancalian and trade Patrian. Carries a
Pavian squire's spear he has no right to.

**Goal:** close the nine Night Roads and give Ancalia back to its living. Smaller and
closer: be forgiven for Hadra, or at least stop running from it.

## Facts

1. Born in the hill country around Hadra, in a family of millers; knows the roads,
   villages and ruins of northern Ancalia like his own hand.
2. For five years a refugee guide: led parties of survivors through the haunted interior
   to the coast, by night roads and dead towns.
3. Was squire to Ser Dawit Senai of the Pavian Order and fled the walls of Hadra the
   night his knight fell. The surviving Pavians name him oathbreaker.

## Words

- **Sun:** sheds daylight at will out to about 60 m; can't be blinded, sees through dark
  and mist; invincible defense against fire.
- **Journeying:** always knows where he is, never loses the way to a known place; travel
  rests and feeds him like sleep and a meal. Its miracles can open or seal Night Roads.
- **Endurance:** needs no food, drink, sleep or breath; Constitution set to 16. Can't stop
  harms that call for a Spirit save.

- **Body of Iron Will:** natural AC 3; immune to natural environmental harm (heat, cold,
  pressure). Armor and shields don't improve it.
- **Defy the Iron:** Commit Effort to the end of the scene: negate one physical attack or
  instance of bodily injury (one round's worth of an ongoing peril). Not mental or spiritual
  harm, nor anything that calls for a Spirit save.
- **Hope of the Dawn:** Commit Effort: he and allies around him get Morale 12 and an
  invincible defense against magical emotional influence; allied NPCs get +1 HD and +1 to
  hit; those who see him sense his wishes.
- **Know the Path:** always knows the safest and easiest way to any place not kept secret;
  those travelling with him count the journey as food and sleep.
- **Sunstrike:** Commit Effort for the scene: the sun's radiance strikes a visible foe for
  1d10 per level (1d6 per level if the sun is down), through any roof. Creatures not of this
  world (summoned things, angels, Uncreated) always take 1d10 per level, rolled twice, the
  worse result counts.
