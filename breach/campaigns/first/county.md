# Harrowdale

The 11th Company's county. The chapter house is in **Kingsmere**. Days are travel from the chapter house. Wards keep a settlement's streets as they were when a breach opens nearby; they are narrative, not a mechanic, and they cover no field or road.

| Settlement | Kind | Days | Known for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kingsmere | city | 0 | a mill and a miller who sells rumours |
| Fenwick | town | 1 | a garrison of six, bored |
| Nether Wick | village | 1 | a monastery that brews |
| Barrowden | village | 2 | a tower, empty, that the ward stone sits in |

## People of the chapter house

The GM writes these as play meets them: the Marshal who sets the board, the chronicler, the quartermaster, the artificer who rebuilds automatons, the Crown's liaison. One line each: who they are and what they want.

- **Marshal Hesketh Vane** (he/him): sets the board in chalk and silence, never a dungeoneer himself; wants the ladder to hold one more year without a gap.
- **Brother Ambrose Tull**, chronicler (he/him): keeps the wall and the college's ledgers; wants every breach described down to the smell, and asks about the rift more than the fight.
- **Maud Pellow**, quartermaster (she/her): counts arrows back in; wants nothing lost that the Crown paid for, and is kinder than that sounds.
- **Isolde Fairweather**, artificer (she/her): rebuilds automatons in a shed that hums; wants the cores brought home, always, and says so to the cores.
- **Sir Lucan Drewe**, the Crown's liaison (he/him): counts breaches, deaths and coin; wants the county's numbers to look like the other counties' numbers.
- **Teg Harrow**, groom and gatekeeper (they/them): old, lame, once a dungeoneer; walked out of a breach and the Company years ago and was let back in to muck stables. Wants the Bull to know that is survivable.

## GM notes

- Month 1: the Bull (Caelynn Brandt, Recruit) and Lampwick sent to a Giant Hyena breach near Hadley, 3 days.

- Hadley (visited month 1, day 5): the ward stone is a squat grey block in the market square, warm to the touch. **Joss Marley**, reeve (he/him): wants the Company to come more often and not only when the board says. **Sister Agnet** of the lazar house (she/her): runs the leper house 400 m outside the ward on the east road, eleven patients; wants someone to come when a breach opens by them, because no one is sent for lepers. **Will Tanner**, farmer (he/him): lost a ewe to the hyena; wants paying for it.
- The Hound, Neega the Younger (Lancer) and Marlow: closed their sabre-tooth breach east of Hadley on day 6, Neega badly clawed; walked home with the Bull. Neega's running bet on who graduates first is with the Wren (Jorund Pendry); the stake is a horse neither of them owns.
- Month 1 end: the Hare (Rowena Lowry) and the Bear (Aelar Vance) on the wall; the Raven graduated. New: the Hare Tana Yarrow (Gnome Monk, with Gaskell), the Bear Nissa Wick (Gnome Wizard, with Sedge), the Raven Alston Greaves (Gnome Sorcerer, Recruit, with Abbott). Caelynn deserted from the Hare's seat eight months ago; Rowena took it.
- Nether Wick's monastery lost a lay brother, Oswy, to the loose denizens of the breach Caelynn walked away from.
- Goose Green (month 2, day 3): the famous inn is the Gridiron, kept by **Hester Quill** (she/her), who bars the doors at dusk since the wraiths came and wants the Company to do something about them that the Company can't. The Aske farm, 2 km out, lost its family of five in one night in month 1's last week; no marks, faces grey. The wraiths keep to night and to the old barrow on Gallow Hill.
- The Heron, **Carlin Lowry** (he/him), Gnome Paladin, Sergeant, with **Tamsin** (Bard automaton, Insight): at the Gridiron on month 2 day 4, bound for his killer whale breach a day east of Goose Green. A Lowry Lane foundling, like Rowena Lowry the dead Hare, whom he called his brother. Labels a knick-knack from every breach. Doesn't blame Caelynn for Rowena; says so.
- Goose Green, month 2: the wraiths came to the edge of the ward two nights ago and stood there till dawn. Copse Wood, 3 km north, has a goblin breach the scouts chalked and no one was sent to (board: level 1, unassigned).
