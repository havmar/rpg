# Harrowdale

The 11th Company's county. The chapter house is in **Kingsmere**. Days are travel from the chapter house. Wards keep a settlement's streets as they were when a breach opens nearby; they are narrative, not a mechanic, and they cover no field or road.

| Settlement | Kind | Days | Known for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kingsmere | city | 0 | a mill and a miller who sells rumours |
| Fenwick | town | 1 | a garrison of six, bored |
| Nether Wick | village | 1 | a monastery that brews |
| Barrowden | village | 2 | a tower, empty, that the ward stone sits in |
| Hollins | village | 2 | a horse fair in spring |
| Goose Green | village | 3 | a famous inn |
| Hadley | town | 3 | a leper house outside the ward |

## People of the chapter house

The GM writes these as play meets them: the Marshal who sets the board, the chronicler, the quartermaster, the artificer who rebuilds automatons, the Crown's liaison. One line each: who they are and what they want.

- **Marshal Hesketh Vane** (he/him): sets the board in chalk and silence, never a dungeoneer himself; wants the ladder to hold one more year without a gap.
- **Brother Ambrose Tull**, chronicler (he/him): keeps the wall and the college's ledgers; wants every breach described down to the smell, and asks about the rift more than the fight.
- **Maud Pellow**, quartermaster (she/her): counts arrows back in; wants nothing lost that the Crown paid for, and is kinder than that sounds.
- **Isolde Fairweather**, artificer (she/her): rebuilds automatons in a shed that hums; wants the cores brought home, always, and says so to the cores.
- **Sir Lucan Drewe**, the Crown's liaison (he/him): counts breaches, deaths and coin; wants the county's numbers to look like the other counties' numbers.
- **Teg Harrow**, groom and gatekeeper (they/them): old, lame, once a dungeoneer; walked out of a breach and the Company years ago and was let back in to muck stables. Wants the Bull to know that is survivable.
