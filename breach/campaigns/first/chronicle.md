# Chronicle of the 11th Company

## Month 1

Caelynn Brandt took the Bull's seat as Recruit on the first of the month and was given a Giant Hyena breach by Hadley, three days out. Her automaton is Lampwick, new from the shed; she is its first partner.

The Bull went out on the first with Lampwick and shared the road to Hadley with the Hound, who was bound for a tiger. On the fourth morning the pasture west of Hadley was grassland that had never been in Harrowdale, and Lampwick went in first.

**The thorn gap.** The Recruit stepped on a dry stick in the gap and four flying snakes came out of their tree before either of the pair could draw. Three bites found her, and she went down with poison in her and a death save already failed. Lampwick, unseen in the thorns, dragged her back round the bend, fumbled the first try at stopping the bleeding and got it on the second, with one failure to spare. She woke an hour later with 1 hit point. The snakes went back to their tree.

**The thorn gap, again.** After a long rest, Lampwick poured oil in the gap and lit it while the Recruit shouted. Her arrow killed the one snake that stayed in the tree, and the other three came at the noise through the fire and didn't come out the other side. A hundred XP, the Recruit's first, and ten gold in the hollow. The hyena in the next room saw Lampwick go to look, and stopped laughing.

**The hyena.** It came at the noise across the snakes' clearing and pushed through the gap, which was too small for it, before anyone else had moved. It stopped on the oil, 4.5 m short of the Recruit. Lampwick was in the thorns beside it and opened its flank. The Recruit marked it and shot it twice. Its second bite put Lampwick on 1 hit point, and the automaton spent the next moments, in its words, attending to not being bitten. The Recruit's third arrow went in behind the shoulder and the hyena went down. Two hundred XP, twenty gold in the bones of room 2, and the rift closed with a sound like a held breath let go. Where it had been there was a hyena's tooth on a cord of plaited grass. Nobody in that breach could have plaited it.

That night, in the corridor of thorns that was turning back into a hedge, the Bull came to the second level.

On the fifth the Bull walked into Hadley. The town was well and its ward stone warm. Outside the ward on the east road the lazar house had frost in its cabbages, blowing in from the Hound's sabre-tooth breach a day further east. Sister Agnet had moved her eleven to the west wall. The Hound had gone through on the third, singing, and hadn't come back yet.

On the sixth the Bull went east to look for the Hound and met him on the road coming back, clawed down one side and laughing about it, with Marlow carrying a sabre-tooth's fang as long as a hand. The frost was going off the lazar house's cabbages by noon. Neega the Younger ate Sister Agnet's soup as promised, and the two pairs walked home to Kingsmere together, arriving on the ninth.

**The rest of the month.** The Prentice-to-be told Brother Ambrose the breach down to the smell of the grass, and he copied out the plaited cord twice. Sister Agnet's message went to the Marshal, who said nothing, and to Sir Lucan, who wrote it down. Will Tanner got his form. Most evenings Lampwick and the Bull sat on the wall walk while it asked her questions about the river, as though it were drawing a map. On the twentieth day Gaskell came home without the Hare, and on the twenty-third Sedge came home without the Bear. Both automatons walked straight to Isolde's shed and stood in it. The Raven left for the Capital on the last day with one bag, and Teg shut the gate after him.

The news of month 1: the Hare, Rowena Lowry, Sergeant-Major, died in the Giant Shark breach by Kingsmere; it was the seat Caelynn walked out of, and he was the one who took it. The Bear, Aelar Vance, Warden, died to wyverns by Hollins. The Raven, Wrenn Ironside, graduated. Five breaches went unhandled and their denizens are loose, the worst of them air elementals a short walk from the city.

## Month 2

The Bull, now Prentice, is given a Giant Goat breach by Goose Green, three days out.

The Prentice spent all but four of her gold on a single healing potion at the apothecary by the cathedral, and Lampwick said it was the first sensible thing anyone had bought in its presence. The road to Goose Green went past the Aske farm, where the wraiths had been. The village sleeps inside its ward now and the Gridiron bars its doors at dusk. On the fourth morning the low fields beyond the village were mountain: scree, thin cold air, and goat bells somewhere above.

**The grimlocks' cave.** Lampwick soaked the cave mouth and lit it, and the grimlocks were cleverer than oil: three of the four went over the fire on the rock above it. The Prentice had already shot one as they came, and shot the one that walked through the flames. The last two crossed 15 m of scree to Lampwick, missed it three times between them, and she finished both with the scimitar. Not a scratch on either body. Two hundred XP, and the Bull is third level by the next rest.

They rested in the grimlocks' cave, and both came out of it third-level. Lampwick went up to the goats' meadow and came down unseen.

**The goats' meadow.** The Prentice charmed the old nanny with the bell and walked her to the rift with a hand on her neck, talking to her in Sylvan, and the other three came after, bells going. The bell-goat stopped at the tear, looked back once at the Prentice, and went through. The rift shut behind the last of them. Nothing killed, nothing absorbed, forty gold in the grass and a cracked bell where the rift had been. Lampwick said it was the best-resolved matter it had attended so far.

At the Gridiron that night the Bull shared a table with the Heron, Carlin Lowry, on his way to a killer whale a day east. He and the dead Hare had grown up together in the Lowry Lane foundling house, and he said he was glad she'd come back, because his brother would have been. He wrote a label for her bell in a small square hand and tied it on.

On the fifth morning Hester Quill unbarred the door and said the wraiths had come to the ward's edge again in the night, three of them by the pound, facing the inn till first light. Beyond the village the mountain was going back to stubble field. The Heron saddled his pony in the yard for the road east.

**Copse Wood.** No one was sent to the goblin breach north of the village, so the Bull went. The copse had become a tall pale wood with primroses in it and a moon overhead at mid-morning. Lampwick went into the hawthorn ring first and found a sunken cistern full of black water, and in the water something large that looked back at it.

**The cistern.** The Prentice went down the steps with a strip of dried beef and let the octopus come up to look at her. It took the beef and the spell together, and when she sat on the bottom step and talked to it, it put the tip of one tentacle on her boot and left it there. Both of the pair walked the ledge past it unharmed. Lampwick, which does not need to breathe, walked down into the black water and came back up with twenty gold off the floor.

**The goblins.** There were four of them behind the arch, bows drawn, in a pale room with the rift humming at the far end. They'd been five until the octopus. Lampwick told them from the archway that the beast was attended to and that the tear behind them went home, and after some argument among themselves the eldest believed it. They left their coins on a stone for the pair, and went through one at a time, the eldest last and walking backwards.

**The octopus.** The Prentice brought it up out of the cistern and across the dry stone on her voice alone, three metres a stride, with Lampwick carrying a bucket of cistern water and pouring it over it every few steps. It nearly balked at the arch. At the rift it touched her boot again and went through. Nothing was killed and nothing absorbed. The rift shut, and where it had been was a primrose pressed flat and dry, as if it had been kept in a book. Lampwick said that was two matters resolved in one day and that it was beginning to feel spoiled.
