# Bastion: The Bell That Rings Tomorrow

A solo tabletop roleplaying campaign inspired by *Electric Bastionland*. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for table rules and the morning reset.

## Character at the first morning anchor

- **Name:** Silas Vane, a placeholder the player may change
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Former work:** municipal bell inspector
- **Debt:** 10,000 crowns owed to the Office of Civic Debts
- **STR 10, DEX 11, CHA 9; HP 3**
- **At 06:00:** work clothes, brass inspection key, pocket notebook, battered lantern
- **Reset:** at will or automatically upon death; the world rewinds to the latest morning anchor, and Silas keeps his memories. No limit or cost.

## Session log

### 001 — The bell beneath Bastion (Day 1, 06:00)

The first sound of the day rises through the floorboards: a deep bell from somewhere under Bastion, although the municipal underground bell registry has been blank for thirty years. Silas Vane wakes in his room above the Copper Kettle. Rain ticks against the window; the brass inspection key in his hand is warm. A debt collector is due at noon. From the stairwell, a woman calls, “Inspector? Tell me that was you.” The player has not yet chosen an action.
