# Bastion: The Bell That Rings Tomorrow

A solo tabletop roleplaying campaign inspired by *Electric Bastionland*. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for table rules and the morning reset.

## Character at the first morning anchor

- **Name:** Silas Vane, a placeholder the player may change
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Former work:** municipal bell inspector
- **Debt:** 10,000 crowns owed to the Office of Civic Debts
- **STR 10, DEX 11, CHA 9; HP 3**
- **At 06:00:** work clothes, brass inspection key, pocket notebook, battered lantern
- **Reset:** at will or automatically upon death; the world rewinds to the latest morning anchor, and Silas keeps his memories. No limit or cost.

## Current anchor and loop ledger

- **Day 1, 06:00:** The rented room above the Copper Kettle. Starting state. Loop 1 has begun.
- **Knowledge carried across loops:** the sound leads to a municipal inspection hatch in the Copper Kettle cellar; its fresh seal is dated tomorrow; the brass inspection key fits its lock.
- **Unresolved leads:** something beneath the hatch makes three slow knocks; water and pressure gather at its rim; a debt collector is due at noon.
- **Promises and relationships:** none established in play.

## Session log

### 001 — The bell beneath Bastion (Day 1, 06:00)

The first sound of the day rises through the floorboards: a deep bell from somewhere under Bastion, although the municipal underground bell registry has been blank for thirty years. Silas Vane wakes in his room above the Copper Kettle. Rain ticks against the window; the brass inspection key in his hand is warm. A debt collector is due at noon. From the stairwell, a woman calls, “Inspector? Tell me that was you.” The player has not yet chosen an action.

Illustration: [001-the-bell-beneath-bastion.webp](bastion-image/001-the-bell-beneath-bastion.webp).

### 002 — The cellar hatch (Day 1, about 06:05)

The player chooses to investigate the bell. Silas follows its vibration downstairs without a roll. The woman from the stairwell is Mrs. Pike, the Copper Kettle's keeper; she brings a lantern. In the wet cellar, he finds a circular municipal inspection hatch. His brass key fits the lock, and the fresh Office of Subterranean Works seal across it is dated tomorrow. The bell falls silent. Three slow knocks sound beneath the iron; water beads at the rim, which flexes upward under pressure. The hatch remains closed and the player has not yet chosen how to proceed.

Illustration: [002-the-cellar-hatch.webp](bastion-image/002-the-cellar-hatch.webp).
