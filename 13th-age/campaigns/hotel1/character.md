# Morten · Level 1 Sorcerer (High Elf) · Battles 0/4 · Advances 0

Morten (he/him). Rolled class: 1d14 = 13, Sorcerer. Race chosen for the Charisma bonus and a
once-per-battle escape: High Elf.

## One Unique Thing

He is the only guest who can hear a brick in a wall, and the only one who can pull it out.

- **AC 15** (10 + middle of Con/Dex/Wis +2 + level 1 + Spell Fist 2) · **PD 14** (11 + middle of Str/Con/Dex +2 + 1) · **MD 12** (10 + middle of Int/Wis/Cha +1 + 1)

- Saves: easy 6+, normal 11+, hard 16+. Death saves 16+.
- Light armour (no penalty). One hand free for spells.

## Racial power

- **Highblood Teleport** (High Elf): once per battle, as a move action, place himself in a nearby location he can see. (Leaves engagement without a disengage check.)

## Class features

- **Gather Power:** in a battle, a standard action (no opportunity attacks) to make the next standard-action sorcerer attack spell **empowered: double damage**, hit or miss. Plus a **chaotic benefit**, 1d6 (adventurer): 1–2 +1 AC until the start of his next turn · 3–4 1 damage to every nearby staggered enemy · 5–6 1 damage to one nearby enemy (energy of his choice: cold, fire, lightning, thunder). Lost if his next standard action isn't a sorcerer attack spell.
- **Dancing Lights:** standard action, coloured light globes 1.5 to 9 m around him; light in a dark room, little control.
- **Breath weapon** (keyword): after casting, roll d20 at the start of each later turn; on the spell's number he may cast it again that turn as a standard action. One active at a time; failing a death save cancels it.
- **Chain** and **random energy** (keywords): random energy 1d4: 1 cold · 2 fire · 3 lightning · 4 thunder.
- **Access to Wizardry** (from level 3).

## Talents (3)

- **Spell Fist:** **+2 AC** (in the AC above). Casts **ranged spells while engaged without opportunity attacks**. Adds **Con (+3)** instead of Cha to all sorcerer spell damage.
- **Arcane Heritage:** +2 to a magic background (the conjurer). One sorcerer spell slot holds a wizard spell of the same level: **Shield**.
- **Sorcerer's Familiar:** **Mrs Pell**, a small grey hotel cat. Permanent ability: **Counter-bite**. Two random abilities, rerolled at each full heal-up: **Talkative** and **Scout** (since Day 1).
  - *Counter-bite:* each battle, if she's close, she bites the first enemy that hits him with a melee attack, after that attack: **1d4 damage** (1d4 per level), no roll.
  - *Talkative:* she talks like a person; the GM voices her. She knows what Morten knows.
  - *Scout:* once a day she slips off and reconnoitres an area (a room, through the gap under its door): an easy skill check for the environment (campaign ruling, `rules.md`).

## Spells (4 at level 1)

All sorcerer spell attacks: **Cha + level = +5** vs the defence, **+ the escalation die**. Damage adds **Con +3** (Spell Fist).

- **Chaos Bolt** (ranged, at-will). One nearby enemy, or one far away at −2. **+5 vs PD.** Hit: **1d8 + 3** random energy (type rolled at the first cast each battle, fixed for that battle); on a natural even hit, a **chaotic benefit** as if he had gathered power. Miss: 1 damage (his level).
- **Burning Hands** (close-quarters, at-will). Up to two nearby enemies in a group. **+5 vs PD.** Hit: **1d6 + 3** fire.
- **Breath of the White** (close-quarters, **daily**, breath weapon 16+). 1d2 nearby enemies in a group. **+5 vs PD.** Hit: **3d6 + 3** cold. Miss: half damage. Re-use on 16+ at the start of each later turn.
- **Shield** (wizard spell via Arcane Heritage; close-quarters, **recharge 11+** after battle). Free action when an attack hits his AC: the attacker rerolls and he must take the new result.

## Feat (1 adventurer)

- **Spell Fist (adventurer):** when he misses with a sorcerer spell against an enemy **engaged with him**, add his **Cha mod (+4)** to the damage. (Chaos Bolt engaged miss: 1 + 4 = 5 damage; Burning Hands engaged miss: 4 damage, `rules.md`.)

## Basic attacks

- **Ranged (thrown dagger):** Dex + level = **+3 vs AC** · hit 1d4 + 2 · miss none.
