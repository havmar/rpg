# Rules for hotel1

Public. These win over `13th-age/CLAUDE.md` and `13th-age/hotel.md` where they differ.

- **The Hotel:** the **Hotel Meridian**. Seed **64** (`roll.py 1d100`), all ten floors generated with `gen_hotel.py --seed 64`.
- **Started:** Day 1 · 08:00, room 100.

## Rulings

- **Familiar's random abilities.** Rolled 1d8 on the wizard's list less Counter-bite (1 Agile, 2 Alert, 3 Flight, 4 Mimic, 5 Poisonous, 6 Scout, 7 Tough, 8 Talkative). A duplicate is rerolled; **Mimic** is rerolled (he has no allies); **Tough** counts as two abilities and is rerolled when only one slot is left.
- **Scout.** Once a day, one turn: Mrs Pell slips under a door. "An easy skill check for the environment" is the tier's **normal** DC (15 at adventurer); Morten rolls d20 + Dex + level + a sneaking background. Success: Morten learns the room's guests (as if he had seen them: stat lines open), what they're doing, and the furniture. Failure falls forward: she sees them, but they see her, and the room's reaction roll takes −1.
- **Spell Fist feat on a spell with no miss damage** (Burning Hands): a miss against an enemy engaged with him still deals his Cha mod, 4 damage.
- **Chaos Bolt's energy** is rolled once a battle (1d4: cold, fire, lightning, thunder) and holds for that battle.
- **Initiative ties** go to Morten.
- **Ongoing damage** from the same creature renews rather than stacks; from different creatures it stacks (each saved separately).
- **A hummed room** (a bar rumour, a Resident, the event): its brick roll is a brick.
- **A guest that wanders** (a wanderer that ignores him) is out of its room for 2d6 turns.
- **Fighting in a searched room** (guests he left alive, or new ones) is a battle; searching it again is not.
- **Coaxing a beast:** the Hotel porter's child background counts (a porter handles guests' pets). A wary beast is a hard check.
- **A failed lock** falls forward: it opens after half an hour (3 turns).
