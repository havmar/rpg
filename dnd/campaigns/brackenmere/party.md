# Party

Two PCs, level 1. Brin is the AI player's PC (`rpg-player` subagent). Sela is run by the DM.

## House rules

- **Death:** a PC who dies stays dead unless the DM finds a story reason to bring them back (a
  priest, a bargain, a god's favor). No free revival.
- **Advancement:** the DM levels the party up at scene ends when they earn it.

## Brin Ironhand (AI): dwarf fighter, level 1

**Looks:** Short, broad, a red beard in two braids, a dented chain shirt, and a shield with a
faded anvil on it.

**Personality:** Gruff, loyal, quick to laugh at his own jokes. Hums old dwarven work songs when
nervous. Distrusts nobles and officials on sight.

**Want:** To earn enough to open a forge of his own, and to prove his grandmother's songs are
worth remembering.

**Flaw:** Can't walk away from a bet or a bully.

- **HP:** 13. **AC:** 19 (chain mail 16, shield +2, Defense +1). **Speed:** 7.5 m (25 ft).
- **Initiative:** +1. **Passive Perception:** 13.
- **Saves:** Str +4, Con +5.
- **Skills:** Athletics (+4), Perception (+3), Intimidation (+1).
- **Attacks:**
  - Longsword: +4, 1d8+2 slashing (versatile 1d10+2).
  - Handaxe (thrown, range 6/18 m, 20/60 ft): +4, 1d6+2 slashing.
- **Features:** Darkvision 18 m (60 ft). Dwarven resilience (advantage on saves against
  poison, resistance to poison damage). Fighting style: Defense. Second Wind (bonus action,
  1d10+1 HP, once per short or long rest).
- **Gear:** chain mail, shield, longsword, handaxe, explorer's pack, a miner's pick, 15 gp.

## Sela Brightwater: half-elf cleric, level 1 (run by the DM)

**Looks:** Tall, silver-blonde hair tied back, a patched blue cloak, and a holy symbol of a
sun-ring on a cord around her neck.

**Personality:** Calm and kind, a little too eager to heal everyone. Asks questions before
she swings a mace. Dry humor when things go badly.

**Want:** To keep the village safe, and to earn her temple's trust again.

**Flaw:** Finds it hard to say no to someone in pain.
