# World: Brackenmere

Facts the players know. Add to this only when the party learns something in play.

- **Brackenmere** is a river village of about 200 people, at the edge of the Greywood. Its one
  inn is the Crooked Lantern.
- **Tomas Greyl** runs the mill on the river. His daughter **Wenna** (11) lives with him.
- **Mayor Orrin Vale** runs the village from the town hall. He has two town guards.
- The mill's wheel stopped at midnight. Nobody in the village saw anything.
