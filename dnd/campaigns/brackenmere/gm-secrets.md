# GM secrets: brackenmere

Only the DM reads this file. The DM's PC (Sela) can't use these facts in play until she
discovers them in the story.

## The truth

- **Wenna Greyl** (11, the miller's daughter) is alive. She was taken from the mill loft
  at midnight by goblins through the old sluice tunnel under the mill.
- The goblins work for **Mayor Orrin Vale**. He wants the mill cheap, and he paid Snik Ratchet
  to make the miller frightened. A letter in Snik's chest, signed "O.V." with a bee seal,
  proves it.
- Snik was told only to scare Tomas off. Taking the girl was his own idea, to make the miller
  sell in a hurry. He's scared now that it went too far, and that the mayor's men will
  come to collect the goblins' pay and leave no witnesses.

## Map

1. **Mill cellar.** Flour sacks, a broken cart wheel, and a trapdoor under the sacks, half open
   and wet. Small footprints and a goblin-sized bootprint lead to it.
2. **Sluice tunnel.** About 30 m (100 ft) of wet stone, knee-deep in river water, to a grate
   with a bronze bee coin wedged in it (the mayor's seal, dropped by a goblin).
3. **Guard post.** Two goblins with shortbows on a ledge, and a wolf chained at the back. The
   wolf is hungry, not vicious.
4. **Warren hall.** Snik Ratchet (goblin boss) and one goblin. Wenna in a rusted cage. A chest
   with 60 gp and the letter. Snik can be talked down if the party shows him a way out.

## Stats

- Goblin: AC 15, HP 7. Scimitar +4 (1d6+2). Shortbow +4 (1d6+2), range 24 m / 80 ft.
- Snik Ratchet: AC 17, HP 21. Two scimitar attacks +4 (1d6+2 each). Fights to the end
  unless the party offers him a way out.
- Wolf: AC 13, HP 11. Bite +4 (2d4+2). On a hit, Str DC 11 or knocked prone.
- Mayor Orrin Vale: lives in the town hall. Smooth. Denies everything. Has two town guards.

## Possible endings

- Free Wenna and expose Vale: the miller keeps the mill, and the town guard arrests the mayor.
- Snik surrenders with the letter, and the party brings him to the village to testify.
- Snik fights to the death, and the mayor's men show up after the fight to clean up.
